倒 装

完全倒装 · 部分倒装 · 形式倒装 —— 结合上海高考真题

板块：语法 · 倒装 重点：完全倒装 + 部分倒装
导读

倒装是什么

Inversion

英语句子有陈述语序（主语在前、谓语在后）和倒装语序（谓语或谓语的一部分提到主语前）。倒装按功能分语法倒装（强制性）和修辞倒装（为了平衡、衔接、描绘）；按形式分三种——形式倒装、完全倒装、部分倒装。

一

形式倒装

主谓顺序没根本变化
感叹句 / the more / whatever
二

完全倒装

主语和谓语完全颠倒
口诀「有-时-表-地-方」
三

部分倒装

助/系/情态动词提前
口诀「不-只-让步-也-常-如此-虚拟-祝福」
🧭
一眼分辨：看句首把谁提到主语前——整个谓语提前是完全倒装（In came the teacher.）；只有助动词/系动词/情态动词提前、动词还在主语后面的是部分倒装（Never have I seen it.）。
一 · 形式倒装

形式倒装：主谓顺序不变

Formal Inversion

由于结构需要改变了「主谓宾 / 主系表」语序，但主谓顺序没有根本变化。主要有三类：

1

感叹句

What + (a/an) + adj. + n. + 主谓
How + adj./adv. + 主谓
What a beautiful day (it is)!
How fast time flies!
How colorful the picture is!
2

the more…the more

The + 比较级 + 正常语序，
the + 比较级 + 正常语序
The sooner, the better.
The greener the trees are, the more beautiful the hill is.
3

whatever / however 从句

Whatever + n. + 主谓, 主句
However + adj./adv. + 主谓, 主句
Whatever you do, you should be brave and careful.
However hard I tried, I could not solve the problem.
💡
定位：这三类里其实没有「主谓颠倒」，只是把某个成分（表语、比较级、whatever/however 短语）提到了前面，所以叫形式倒装。高考里它通常作为翻译或写作的「加分结构」出现。
二 · 完全倒装

完全倒装：五字诀「有-时-表-地-方」

Full Inversion

主语和谓语完全颠倒——整个谓语动词（实义动词）直接跑到主语前面。五种情况用一句话记：

有

There be 结构

be 可由 exist / seem / happen / appear / live / stand 等替代
时

now / then 句首

Now/Then + come(be/go/lie/run) + 主语
表

表语前置

形容词/过去分词/介词短语 + 系动词 + 主语
地

地点副词/介词短语句首

here / there / on the wall / under the tree…
方

方向副词句首

out / in / up / down / away…
🔑
一句话判断：完全倒装里，句首通常是地点、方向、时间或表语，谓语是一个不及物动词（come、go、lie、stand、fly、run、exist 等），整个动词提前。主语往往较重（有定语修饰），倒装让句子更平衡。
二 · 完全倒装

有 · 时 · 表

There / Now / Predicative
① 有 — There be（替代动词）
There exist different answers to this question.
There happened to be two apples in the bag.
There stood a dog before him.
be 的替代动词：exist / seem / happen / appear / live / rise / stand。
② 时 — now / then 句首
Then came the hour we had been looking forward to.
Now comes your turn.
结构：Now/Then + come(或 be、go、lie、run 等) + 主语。
③ 表 — 表语前置（形容词）
Present at the meeting were Professor White and many other guests.
③ 表 — 过去分词 / 介词短语
Gone are the days when the small town was quiet.
Among the goods are Christmas trees, flowers, candles and toys.
表语前置形容词 / 过去分词 / 介词短语 + 系动词 + 主语
💡
观察点：表语前置时，系动词 be 放在表语后、主语前（Present were guests / Gone are the days），这是「主系表」整体调头，不是助动词提前。
二 · 完全倒装

地 · 方（+ 三个注意点）

Place / Direction
④ 地 — 地点副词/介词短语句首
Here comes the old lady!
At the foot of the hill lies a beautiful lake.
On the top of which flew a flag.（定语从句内）
East of the lake lie two towns.
Under the tree lay a wounded farmer.
⑤ 方 — 方向副词句首
In came Mr. White.
Up went the arrow into the air.
Away went the boy.
直接引语在句首
"You must be joking!" shouted my wife.
"No way," said my boss coldly.
⚠️
高频陷阱：完全倒装中如果主语是人称代词，就不倒装——Here you are.（给你）/ There she comes.（她来了）。只有名词主语才倒装：Here comes the bus.（车来了）
三 · 部分倒装

部分倒装：八字诀总览

Partial Inversion

只把助动词、系动词或情态动词提到主语前，谓语动词本身仍留在主语后。八种情况用一句话串起来：

不

否定词句首

never / not / little
hardly / seldom…
只

only + 状语句首

only then / only by…
only when…
让步

as / though 从句

表语 / 状语 / 动词前置
也

so / neither / nor

So + 助/情 + 主语
常

频度副词句首

often / many a time…
如此

so / such…that

so、such 置于句首
虚拟

were / had / should

省略 if 的虚拟句
祝福

祝愿句

May… / Long live…
🔑
口诀：「不一只一让一步，也一常一如一此一虚拟一祝福」——句首出现否定、only、让步、so/neither、频度、so/such…that、虚拟 were/had/should、祝愿词时，用部分倒装。前四组（不/只/让步/也）是高考主战场。
三 · 部分倒装

「不」— 否定词 / 半否定词句首

Negative Adverbs
常见否定词：never、not、no、few、seldom、rarely、little、hardly、scarcely、nowhere、no longer　常见否定短语：in no way、at no time、in no case、by no means、on no condition、under/in no circumstances
否定词句首
Not a word did I say to him.
Never have I found him so happy.
Little does he care about what I said.
Seldom does he go out alone at night.
否定短语句首
In no way should you give up.
By no means is he the best player.
Under no circumstances will we give in.
At no time did she lose heart.
🔑
操作：句首是否定词 → 在主语前补助动词/情态动词/系动词，谓语动词变原形。注意半否定词（little、seldom、rarely、hardly、scarcely）表示「几乎不」，同样触发倒装，这是语法填空的高频考点。
三 · 部分倒装

「不」— 四个否定句型

Negative Patterns
① Not only…but also（前倒后不倒）
Not only does John love Chinese, he is also good at speaking it.
连接主语时不倒装：Not only the mother but also the children are sick.
② Not until + 状语/从句（主倒从不倒）
Not until I began to work did I realize how much time I had wasted.
主句倒装，until 从句不倒装。
③ No sooner…than / Hardly…when（前半倒）
No sooner had we reached home than it began to rain.
Hardly had we reached home when it began to rain.
前半句倒装，后半句不倒装。
④ Neither…nor（前后都倒）
Neither will he go to the party, nor does he want to go.
前后两个分句都倒装。
⚠️
别搞混：Not only…but also 是「前倒后不倒」；Not until 是「主倒从不倒」；No sooner…than / Hardly…when 是「前半倒后半不倒」；Neither…nor 是「前后都倒」。四个句型各记一句即可。
三 · 部分倒装

「只」— only + 状语句首

Only + Adverbial

「only + 状语」（副词、介词短语、状语从句）置于句首时，主句用部分倒装。

only + 副词
Only then did I realize the importance of math.
直到那时我才意识到数学的重要性。
only + 介词短语
Only by this means is it possible to explain it.
只有通过这种方法才能解释清楚。
only + 状语从句
Only when the war was over was he able to get back to work.
主句倒装，从句不倒装。
⚠️
唯一例外：only 修饰主语时不倒装——Only he can answer the question.（只有他能回答这个问题）。判断方法：only 后面直接跟的是名词/代词做主语，就不倒装；跟的是状语，就倒装。
三 · 部分倒装

「让步」— as / though 引导让步从句

as / though Concession

此句型主谓并不倒装，只是把表语 / 状语 / 谓语动词提到 as / though 前面。作表语的单数可数名词提前时不带冠词。

① 表语前置
Exhausted as she was, she wasn't able to sleep.
Strange though it seems, it is true.
Child as he is, he knows much about football.
单数名词提前不带冠词：Child as he is（不是 A child）。
② 状语前置
Much as he likes the bike, he doesn't want to buy it.
Carefully as he did his homework, he still made many mistakes.
副词提到 as 前。
③ 谓语动词前置
Try as he might, he didn't pass the exam.
Fail as he did, he didn't give up.
动词原形提到 as 前，主语后补助动词。
💡
翻译抓手：「尽管……但……」用 名词/形容词/副词/动词原形 + as/though + 主谓。这是上海卷翻译题里「让步」表达的高级句式。
三 · 部分倒装

「也」— so / neither / nor 开头

so / neither / nor
① So + be/助/情态 + 主语（表示「也如此」）
He has been to Canada. So have I.
他去过加拿大，我也去过。
— I was a teacher but I want to be a doctor now.
— So it is with my friend.（也可说 It is the same with…）
② Neither / Nor + 助/情 + 主语（表示「也不」）
He doesn't like coffee. Neither/Nor do I.
他不喜欢咖啡，我也不喜欢。
If Joe's wife doesn't go, neither will he.
⚠️
倒装 vs 不倒装：So + 主语 + 助/情态（主语在前）表示「确实如此」，肯定前面的话——So he has.（他确实去了）。对比 So + 助/情态 + 主语（倒装）表示「也如此」——So have I.（我也去了）。这是选择题的经典陷阱。
三 · 部分倒装

「常 · 如此 · 虚拟 · 祝福」

Four More
「常」— often / many a time / now and again 句首
Many a time has John given me good advice.
Often have we taken that test.
「如此」— so / such…that（so、such 句首）
So fast did he run that I could not catch him.
Such a good boy is he that we all like him.
so/such 提到句首，主句倒装，that 从句不倒装。
「虚拟」— were / had / should 省略 if
Were I you, I would go abroad.（= If I were you）
Had you come yesterday, you would have seen him.
Should he come, tell him to ring me up.
「祝福」— 祝愿句
May you succeed!
Long live the People's Republic of China!
may 或 long live 置于句首。
🔑
前四组是重点：「常、如此、虚拟、祝福」里，「虚拟（were/had/should 省 if）」和「如此（so…that 句首）」更常见于书面语和写作；「常、祝福」认识即可。
四 · 其他倒装结构

拓展：你可能不知道的倒装

More Inversion
① 副词/介词 + with + 名词（表命令）
In with you, boys!（都进去）
Up with the flowers, Tom!（把花捡起来）
Out with your ideas!（说出想法）
② 行为动词 + 主语 + 情态/助动词（强调）
It's my duty to help you out, and do it I will.
Go ahead I must. I won't give up.
③ of 短语句首（过长时前置）
Of the thirty students, as many as ten passed the interview.
④ 比较 / 方式状语从句（than / as 倒装）
Mothers usually give more than will fathers.
He is honest and motivated, as are most high-achieving students.
Nowhere is this more evident than in the United States.
💡
这部分是拔高拓展，能看懂即可，高考重点是前面的完全倒装和部分倒装。但 ④ 里的 as 引导的定语/方式从句倒装（as are/do/does…）在阅读和写作中很实用。
五 · 真题精练

上海高考真题（倒装考点）

Gaokao Practice
1 · 语法填空（2018 春考）
I had expected to have more free time after my sons left for college… The kitchen calendar looks spare. Rarely ___ I need to prepare family dinner every day.
▸ 点击查看答案
do
考点：半否定词 rarely 句首，部分倒装，补助动词 do。
2 · 语法填空（2017 春考）
Not only ___ the writers speak with people who study culture and group behavior, but also they talked with animal experts like zookeepers.
▸ 点击查看答案
did
考点：Not only…but also，前句倒装后句不倒装，过去时补 did。
3 · 翻译（2019 春考）
让我的父母非常满意的是，从这个公寓的餐厅可以俯视街对面的世纪公园，从起居室也可以。(so)
▸ 点击查看答案
…overlooks the Century Park opposite the street and so it is with the sitting room.／…, so can they from the living room.
考点：so 表示「也」，用 So + 助/情 + 主语 的倒装。
4 · 写作范文（2018 春考）
So appealing is your plan to introduce a robot to our school that I decide to respond to your asking for proposals.
▸ 点击查看答案
（so…that 结构）
考点：so 置于句首，主句部分倒装（So appealing is…），that 从句不倒装。
5 · 语境识别（2017 秋考）
Not only do people seem more anxious when animals are nearby, but they may also live longer.
▸ 点击查看答案
（Not only do… 已体现倒装）
考点：阅读中识别 Not only + 倒装结构，帮助理解句意。
6 · 语境识别（2023 春考）
Smith and others knew that the succession of life forms preserved as fossils is useful… Only later did scientists develop a theory to explain that succession.
▸ 点击查看答案
（Only + 状语 → 倒装）
考点：Only + 副词 later 句首，主句倒装（did scientists develop）。
收尾

本章小结

Summary

完全倒装（有-时-表-地-方）

  • 有：There be（可换 exist / stand / lie 等）。
  • 时：now / then 句首。
  • 表：形容词 / 过去分词 / 介词短语作表语前置。
  • 地 / 方：地点、方向副词/介词短语句首。
  • 注意：人称代词主语不倒装（Here you are）。

部分倒装（不-只-让步-也-常-如此-虚拟-祝福）

  • 不：否定词/短语、Not only、Not until、No sooner…than。
  • 只：only + 状语（修饰主语不倒装）。
  • 让步：表语/状语/动词原形 + as/though。
  • 也：So/Neither/Nor + 助情 + 主语。
  • 虚拟：were / had / should 省 if 提前。
⚠️
最易失分的五处：① 人称代词主语误倒装（Here you are 不倒装）；② only 修饰主语误倒装；③ Not only 后句也倒装（应前倒后不倒）；④ so 表示「确实如此」误倒装（So he has）；⑤ Child as he is 误带冠词。
先看句首是「地点方向 / 否定 only / 让步 so 也」· 再看提前的是「整个谓语还是助动词」 —— 倒装，一个框架拿下
