主谓一致：一条主线 · 三个原则

Subject-Verb Agreement

主谓一致指句子的主语和谓语在人称和数方面保持一致。高考考查的核心是：名词单/复数、不可数名词、不定代词、并列结构、特殊名词作主语时，谓语该用单数还是复数。

一

语法一致

看形式：主语是单数，谓语用单数；主语是复数，谓语用复数。
二

意义一致

看含义：形式上是复数、意义上是单数（或反之）时，按意义定谓语。
三

就近一致

看位置：谓语与最靠近它的那个名词在人称和数上保持一致。
🧭
一眼分辨：先看主语「算一个还是算一群」，再看它「离谓语近不近」。意义一致决定多数题目，就近/就远原则只在特定句型（either…or、there be、as well as 等）里登场。
一 · 复数主语

「长得不像复数、却永远用复数」

Always Plural
考点1 · police / cattle
Police are looking for the man.
Many cattle are suffering from the disease.
作主语一律用复数，且不能用数字修饰（不能说 two police）；「两名警察」说 two police officers。
考点2 · the + 国籍形容词
The British / Dutch / English / French… are…
the British 等结构指「全体英国人」，作复数用。「一个荷兰人」用 a Dutchman，不能说 a Dutch。
考点3 · 两部分构成的事物
Your jeans / trousers / glasses / scissors… are…
由两部分构成的东西作复数；说「一件」用 a pair of + 单数动词。
口诀police、cattle、the+国籍形容词、成双成对物 —— 见它们，谓语一律复数
⚠️
易错点：a pair of 后面若说的是两个不同的东西（不是一对），谓语用复数——A pair of nail-scissors is necessary.（一把）／ A pair of pumpkins are in the window.（两个南瓜）。
二 · 集合名词

集合名词：重整体 or 重个体

Collective Nouns

family、team、government、committee、couple 等：被看成「一个单位」用单数，被看成「成员个体」用复数。

重个体 → 复数
His family have decided to move to New York.
The team are full of enthusiasm.
强调「每个成员各自参与」（做决定、希望、要求等）时用复数。
重整体 → 单数
The average British family has 3.6 members.
A team which is full of enthusiasm wins.
被看成一个单位、与单数限定词（a、each、every、this、that）连用时用单数。
定语从句中 who / which 的分工
The committee, who are hoping to announce…（成员）
The committee, which is elected at the meeting.（单位）
couple 的两种用法
The couple lives in apartment 9A.（一对夫妻→整体）
The couple are going to Shanghai and Beijing.（强调个体）
💡
判断抓手：看谓语后面的动作是「集体一起做」还是「各自分别做」。一起 → 单数；各自 → 复数。写作中用 family/team 时，保持同一句内单复数一致（The team is… It expects…）。
三 · 数量表达

a number of / the number of / majority / half

Quantity
a number of / a couple of / a group of / a total of + 复数名词 → 复数动词
A number of people have tried to find the treasure.
A total of 15 countries have signed the agreement.
the number of / the total (of) + 名词 → 单数动词
The number of children who drop out is astounding.
注意：the number of + 名词 + 定语从句，从句里的动词用复数（who drop out），核心谓语用单数（is）。
the/a majority of / half of + 可数复数 → 复数
The majority of students are interested.
Half of the cats have escaped.
the/a majority of / half of + 不可数 → 单数
The majority of the work has been completed.
Half of the milk is drunk.
规律a number of → 复数 · the number of → 单数 · majority/half 看后面名词（可数复数→复数，不可数→单数）
🔑
写作加分：The number of … is/has been … 是描述「数据/趋势」的高频句式；A majority of … 用来表达「大多数人的看法」。
三 · 数量表达

all / any · 度量单位 · 计算

all · Measure · Math
考点6 · all / any
All I eat is lettuce.（指物→单数）
All are here.（指人→复数）
all 指事物 = everything（单数）；指人 = all of them（复数）。any 同理。
考点7 · 度量单位
Twenty miles is a long way to walk.
Where is that five pounds I lent you?
数字 + 度量单位（钱、距离、时间等）看作一个整体，用单数。
考点8 · 计算
Two and two is/are four.
Ten times five is/are fifty.
算式作主语，单复数均可，单数更常见。
⚠️
高频陷阱：「时间/距离/金钱」作主语，即使数字大于一，也是单数——Three hours is enough.（三小时够了）这是语法填空和单选里最爱考的一类。
四 · 单数陷阱

more than one · and 连接两个名词

More than one · and
考点9 · more than one + 单数名词 → 单数动词
More than one person is going to find a new job.
唯一例外：指「一个词的复数形式」时用复数（more than one are animals）。
考点10 · and 连接的两词看成一个概念 → 单数
Your toast and marmalade is on the table.
The spaghetti and meatballs is comfort food.
bread and butter、fish and chips、law and order、whisky and soda 等固定搭配看作一个整体。
and 连接两个不相干的单数名词 → 复数
Dedication and industry experience help students succeed.
一个抽象名词被两个形容词修饰 → 复数
Eastern and Western art differ in many ways.
指「东、西两种不同的艺术」→ 复数。
💡
判断线索：两个名词用 and 连接，能合成「一件事/一种东西」（一种食物、一个概念）→ 单数；各指各的 → 复数。
四 · 单数陷阱

国家/组织名称 · 以 -ics 结尾的词

Nations & -ics
考点11 · 复数形式的国家/组织名 → 单数
The United States is anxious to improve its image.
Consolidated Fruitgrowers has taken over the company.
The United States、the United Nations 等，形式上复数、意义是「一个国家/组织」→ 单数。
考点12 · -ics 结尾的词 → 通常单数
Mathematics is a very important subject.
Statistics is useful in language testing.
mathematics、physics、politics、athletics 通常不可数单数；被具体化后可作复数（What are your politics?）。
⚠️
对比记忆：The statistics are disturbing.（统计数据，复数）vs Statistics is useful.（统计学，单数）。词本身没变，意义变了单复数就变——这正是「意义一致」原则。
五 · 就近就远

就近原则：either…or · there be

Proximity
考点13 · either…or / neither…nor / not only…but also
Either a table or two chairs have got to be moved.
Neither she nor I have arrived.
谓语与 or / nor / but also 后面的名词保持一致（就近）。
考点14 · there be 句型
There is a fly in my soup!
There are some trees and a cottage behind our school.
be 与第一个名词保持一致（就近）。倒装句同理：On the table is a cake.
🔑
但 neither of / either of（后面不跟并列名词）时，动词一律单数——Neither of my brothers has been outside England. Has either of them been seen recently?
五 · 就近就远

one of … · as well as …

one of · Distance
考点15 · one of + 复数名词 + who/that… → 复数动词
She's one of the few women who have climbed Mt. Qomolangma.
This is one of those books that are read by everybody.
但 the only one of the… → 单数（就远）：This is the only one of the cars that never breaks down.
考点16 · 就远原则（插入成分不影响谓语）
The boss, accompanied by his assistants, was coming toward me.
The problem, along with its consequences, arises.
along with / as well as / together with / like / no one but / nobody except … 后面的动词看前面的主语。
口诀as well as、together with、along with、accompanied by、like、but/except —— 夹在中间不算数，看主语本身
⚠️
经典陷阱：The teacher together with the students is…（就远，看 teacher）—— 千万不要被中间的 students 带跑。这是高考单选题的「送分题也是送命题」。
六 · 特殊主语

年代 · who · what

Decades · who · what
考点17 · 年代作主语 → 复数
The 1930s were a tough time in America.
考点18 · who 对主语提问 → 单数
Who is working tomorrow?
即使答案可能是复数，问句也用单数；但 who 对表语提问时看表语（Who are your friends?）。
考点19 · what 引导的主语从句
What is needed is a return to terms.
What worries them are customers like Eric.
what 指单数概念→单数；指复数概念→复数；表抽象整体（含复数义）→ 常单数。
💡
写作抓手：用 What + 主语 + 谓语 + is/are + 表语 作主语从句，是强调句式的替代写法——What matters most is…（最重要的是……）。
六 · 特殊主语

each / everyone · data / media

each · data
考点20 · each (of) / everyone / every one (of)
Each (of them) is required to speak for five minutes.
They each are required to speak.（each 在复数名词后→复数）
everyone/every one (of) 永远单数；kind/sort/type 同理（This kind of medicine is… / Medicines of this kind are…）。
考点21 · data / media 常用作单数
The data is collected.（也可 these data are…）
The media is…（the media 常作不可数单数）
data 现多用不可数单数；media 表「不同媒体方式」时才用复数。agenda、opera 等也作单数。
⚠️
易错点：every one of them is（every one 分开写）≠ everyone is（连写）。写成 every one of + 复数名词时，谓语仍用单数。
六 · 特殊主语

单复数同形 · none · population

Invariable · none · population
考点22 · 单复数同形的词
The company's headquarters was designed by G.
The two companies' headquarters were on opposite sides.
sheep、deer、fish、series、species、headquarters、means 等拼写不变，看「指单个还是多个」定动词。
考点23 · none of 结构
None of the students is/are absent.
None of the information is accurate.
none of + 可数复数：单复数均可（强调「无一」用单数）；none of + 不可数：必须单数。
考点24/25 · population · one and a half
The population of China is huge.
One and a half bananas are left on the table.
population 常作单数；one and a half + 复数名词 通常复数（数量大于一），但作整体概念时也可单数。
🔑
规律总结：单复数同形、none of、population 这三类，无一例外都要「看意义」——指一个就单数，指多个就复数。这再次印证了意义一致原则是主谓一致的第一原则。
七 · 真题精练

单项选择（真题风格）· 上

Practice 1
1
The factory used 65 percent of the raw materials, the rest of which ____ saved for other purposes.
▸ 答案
D. were
考点：the rest of which 指 materials（复数）。
2
One third of the country ____ covered with trees and the majority of the citizens ____ black people.
▸ 答案
A. is, are
考点：country 不可数→is；citizens 复数→are。
3
Barbara is easy to recognize as she's the only one of the women who ____ evening dress.
▸ 答案
B. wears
考点：the only one of… → 定语从句动词单数。
4
Listening to loud music at rock concerts ____ caused hearing loss in some teenagers.
▸ 答案
C. has
考点：动名词短语作主语→单数。
5
Such poets as Shakespeare ____ widely read, of whose works, however, some ____ difficult to understand.
▸ 答案
A. are, are
考点：such poets as→复数；some (works)→复数。
6
The number of foreign students attending Chinese universities ____ rising steadily since 1990.
▸ 答案
C. has been
考点：the number of→单数；since 1990→完成时。
7
The teacher together with the students ____ discussing Reading Skills that ____ newly published in America.
▸ 答案
D. is, was
考点：together with→就远（teacher 单数）；Reading Skills 书名→单数。
8
Either you or one of your students ____ to attend the meeting that is due tomorrow.
▸ 答案
B. is
考点：either…or 就近 → one of your students（单数）。
9
Dr. Smith, together with his wife and daughters, ____ visit Beijing this summer.
▸ 答案
A. is going to
考点：就远原则，看 Dr. Smith（单数）。
10
— Did you go to the show last night? — Yeah. Every boy and girl in the area ____ invited.
▸ 答案
D. was
考点：every…and every… → 单数。
11
Afterwards, we found that an apple and a half ____ on the table.
▸ 答案
B. were left
考点：one and a half + 名词 → 复数。
12
We live day by day, but in terms of history, the time of days and weeks ____ so small that a day is unimportant.
▸ 答案
A. is
考点：time 作整体概念 → 单数。
七 · 真题精练

单项选择（真题风格）· 下

Practice 2
13
As a result of the serious flood, two-thirds of the buildings in the area ____ need repairing.
▸ 答案
A. need repairing
考点：buildings 复数 → need；need doing = 需要被修。
14
A survey of the opinions of experts ____ that three hours of outdoor exercise a week ____ good for one's health.
▸ 答案
B. shows, is
考点：A survey 单数；three hours 整体单数。
15
Professor James will give us a lecture on the Western culture, but when and where ____ yet.
▸ 答案
A. hasn't been decided
考点：when and where 看成一个整体 → 单数。
16
The company had about 20 notebook computers but only one-third ____ used regularly.
▸ 答案
D. were
考点：one-third (of computers) 复数 → were。
17
In fact, not only Mary and I but also Jane ____ having one exam after another.
▸ 答案
C. is tired of
考点：not only…but also 就近 → Jane 单数；be tired of。
18
Butter and bread ____ their daily food, but the bread and the butter ____ up now.
▸ 答案
D. is, have been eaten
考点：butter and bread 整体→单数；分开（the…and the…）→复数。
19
An average of 200 letters a week ____ received by the newspaper's office. Such ____ the case with us at present.
▸ 答案
A. are; is
考点：an average of + 复数→are；such 作主语→is。
20
It's said that the team ____ twelve top European players.
▸ 答案
A. consists of
考点：team 看作整体→单数；consist of 无被动。
21
Sixty miles ____ in a single night, so they were exhausted.
▸ 答案
B. was covered
考点：距离作整体→单数。
22
____ an English-Chinese dictionary.
▸ 答案
A. The students each have
考点：each 在复数名词后→动词用复数 have。
23
Every possible means ____ to prevent air pollution, but the sky is still not clear.
▸ 答案
D. has been used
考点：means 单复数同形，every 限定时用单数。
24
My family ____ all fond of singing English songs and we can sing ____ than others.
▸ 答案
D. are, much more
考点：family 重个体（all）→are；much 修饰比较级。
七 · 真题精练

高考真题回放（上海卷）

Real Gaokao
语法填空 · 主谓一致（真题风格）
Not only ___ the writers speak with people who study culture and group behavior, but also they talked with animal experts.
▸ 点击查看答案
did
考点：Not only…but also 前句部分倒装 + 主谓一致，过去时补 did。
语法填空 · 主谓一致（真题风格）
Only later ___ scientists develop a theory to explain that succession.
▸ 点击查看答案
did
考点：Only + 状语 句首倒装，主语 scientists 复数，补助 did。
翻译 · 2022 秋考
每逢节假日，我们都会去郊区露营，为的就是享受悠闲惬意的生活。（so that）
▸ 点击查看答案
On every holiday, we go camping in the suburbs so that we can enjoy a leisurely and comfortable life.
考点：主语 we 复数，一般现在时。
翻译 · 2023 秋考
这部电视连续剧的剧情围绕某一虚拟的华南城市展开，在剧中汽车工业一直是该城市的骄傲。（where）
▸ 点击查看答案
The plot of this TV series revolves around a fictional city in South China, where the automobile industry has always been the pride of the city.
考点：where 引导非限制性定语从句，先行词 city 单数。
💡
实战提醒：上海卷主谓一致主要藏在语法填空（Section A）和翻译里。语法填空靠「找主语 + 判断单复数 + 定谓语形式」三步走；翻译则要确保每个分句的主谓一致，尤其是 where/which 从句的先行词。

本章小结

Summary

一看就「永远复数」

  • police / cattle（不可数修饰）。
  • the + 国籍形容词（the British）。
  • 成双成对物（glasses、scissors、jeans）。
  • 年代（the 1930s）。

二看就「意义一致」

  • 集合名词：整体单数、个体复数。
  • a number of（复数）/ the number of（单数）。
  • majority / half / all：看后面名词。
  • 度量/距离/金钱：整体单数。
  • 单复数同形、none、population：看含义。

三看就「就近/就远」

  • 就近：either…or / neither…nor / not only…but also / there be。
  • 就近：one of + 复数 + who…（用复数）。
  • 就远：as well as / together with / along with / but / except。

四记「特殊主语」

  • who 问主语：用单数。
  • what 从句：看所指代内容。
  • each/every：单数；each 在复数名词后→复数。
  • the only one of：定语从句单数。
⚠️
最易失分的五处：① 集合名词「整体/个体」分不清；② the number of 误用复数；③ 度量单位误用复数；④ 就远原则被中间的复数带跑；⑤ the only one of 误用复数。
先问「主语算一个还是一群」· 再问「离谓语近不近」 —— 主谓一致，三步定谓语
