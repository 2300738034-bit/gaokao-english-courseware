Part 1 · 概述

新高考背景下的听力

Overview
2025

听力与听说测试合并

自 2025 年春季高考开始，上海高考外语考试实施听力与听说测试合并。原高考外语笔试部分的「听力理解」并入听说测试，外语科目考试总时长不变。
机考

由纸笔卷转为机考卷

此次调整最大的变化是听力由纸笔卷转为机考卷，对操作的规范性、处置的及时性等都提出了更高要求。
题型沿革

2017 年，上海高考英语听力由原先三部分——短对话、短文理解、长对话填词（一般一组 2 人，共 7 组对话）——变为现在的两部分：短对话 + 短文理解和长对话，题型均为四选一单选。其中第二部分可能由三段录音组成。

💡
备考方向：平时应多注重语言基础知识积累，注意连读、失爆、清浊辅音等发音规律，同时注重文化背景积累，体会不同场景中事件的前因后果推断、隐含意义判断。
Part 1 · 应试技巧

听力分数提不高的三大原因

Three Problems
音

音的问题

听力是通过耳朵而非眼睛进行，听辨语音是最基础、最本质的问题，包括升调、降调、连读、弱读、失爆等。
词

词汇的问题

每个单词都听懂、都认识，却不知道句子什么意思——说明对词汇的理解还存在问题。
题

题型的问题

对不同题型没有套路意识，将在下文分题型详解逐一解决。
词汇问题表现在三个方面
① 书面语 ≠ 口语

口语词汇偏短小，句式也没书面语那么复杂。

② 单词 ≠ 习语

习语有固定含义，非字面叠加。如 break a leg 不是「断一条腿」，而是祝对方成功。

③ 偏词 ≠ 常用词

类似 11 选 10 里的「熟词生义」，听力里也可能出现，如 wind up 本意「上发条」，句中常表完成、结束。

Part 1 · 预判

听前预判：三种提高预判效果的方法

Prediction

所谓预判，就是在听音前根据卷面信息预测听音重点、做好预听工作；在听音过程中根据关联词预测。比如：选项中都是时间→ 该题问某一时刻；选项中都是地点→ 问对话发生的场景。

①

联想预判

看到 restaurant 就联想 waiter / order / menu；长对话和段落语篇可通过几个选择项猜出全文主题甚至细节。
②

比较预判

看到选项里出现 thirteen / thirty、fourteen / forty 等音近词，即可猜到本题考查辨音能力。
③

揣摩预判

听力只放一遍，可能没听懂原义；可从出题人角度判断：原文关键词极可能被改写，原文原句很可能不是正确项。
🔑
预判就是「带着问题听」：先扫题干 + 选项锁定考点类型（时间 / 地点 / 人物 / 态度 / 行为 / 推断），再在听音时有针对性地抓关键词、数字、转折、语气。

题型 1 · 数字计算

数字计算题

Number & Calculation
真题复现
A. $200. B. $400. C. $250. D. $500.
听力原文

M: How much did you pay for your new stereo, Joan?

W: I got a great deal on it. They wanted 500 dollars for it at a shop downtown but I found it online for half that price.

Q: How much did the woman pay for the stereo?

▸ 答案 + 解析
C（$250）
考生没听懂 stereo 没关系，只要理解它是一个物品即可。抓住关键信息 $500、half，做简单运算即可：500 ÷ 2 = $250。
技巧点拨

这类题主要检测时间、价格、运算、电话号码等。除对数字十分敏感外，还需注意近似意思的表达，如：two hours and a half behind schedule = to be delayed for two hours and a half = two and a half hours late。要排除同音词 / 近音词干扰，如 nine thirty（9:30）与 nine thirteen（9:13）。

题型 1 · 数字计算

数字题高频词汇

Key Words

要听清楚数字及它们之间的关系。就时间题而言，在把握时间表达法的基础上，先听清时间与事件的关系（关注与时间有关的副词和倍数关系），尤其注意时间上的提前和推后，再正确换算。

A

时间关系词

more / less · late / early · fast / slow · before / after
B

引起数字变化的动词

half / double · miss / postpone · increase / add · decrease —— 加减关系由它们决定。
⚠️
辨音陷阱：抓住近似音、同音词的干扰，例如 nine thirty 表 9:30，而 nine thirteen 表 9:13；thirteen / thirty、fourteen / forty 常被用来设坑。
🔑
做题顺序：① 先听清原始数字；② 再抓修饰数字的动词 / 副词（double、half、late、early）；③ 最后做加减乘除得出答案。
题型 2 · 地点判断

地点判断题

Place / Location
真题复现
A. In a bookstore. B. In a grocery store. C. In a department store. D. In a stationery store.
听力原文

W: I must have your receipt for this shirt in order to exchange it.

M: OK. Here it is.

Q: Where does the conversation most likely take place?

▸ 答案 + 解析
C（In a department store）
对话中听到 receipt（收据）、shirt（衬衫）、exchange（换货）及营业员说的 Here it is.，可推断双方在买卖东西，故选 C。
技巧点拨

抓住对话中的关键词语，但有些词并非明确表示、而是暗示地点。要熟悉与 school / library / restaurant / hospital / hotel / store / supermarket / station / street / home / office / bank 等场所相关的词汇与短语。

题型 3 · 职业与关系

职业与人物关系题

Occupation & Relationship
真题复现2018 年度崇明区一模试卷
A. A journalist. B. An electrician. C. A salesman. D. An operator.
听力原文

W: This is Mrs. Anderson. There's no power in my house. Could you come over and fix it?

M: Don't worry madam. I'll be there in a minute.

Q: What's the probable occupation of the man?

▸ 答案 + 解析
B（An electrician）
听到 power in my house（家里断电）及 fix it（修理），说明家里断电；男士用 madam 等尊称，说明双方关系较远（非家人），故是电工。
技巧点拨

判断人物关系主要看内容、语气、行为和态度，尤其语气。若男士回答 Sorry darling, I will ask for some help. 则可判断是男朋友 / 丈夫；还要留意是否用敬语。专用词汇也能暗示身份：听到 dentist / prescribe / pill → 与医生有关；selling season → 销售员；waitress 会谈 menu / food / order / wine；policeman 会说 tickets / driving license / lights。

题型 4 · 态度评价

态度与评价题

Attitude & Evaluation
真题复现2017 年普通高等学校招生全国统一考试（6 月）
A. They like reading today's paper. B. They are interested in today's paper. C. They found nothing interesting in today's paper. D. They have no idea what the paper is about.
听力原文

M: Just look at today's paper. Nothing but accidents or gossip.

W: Right. Nothing is worth reading.

Q: What is the speaker's attitude towards today's paper?

▸ 答案 + 解析
C
女士说报纸除了事故和小道消息没有别的内容、很无聊；男士说 Right 表示肯定，并重复 Nothing is worth reading，故两人都觉得没什么可读的。
技巧点拨

判断双方对谈及事物的态度：赞赏 / 反对 / 关心 / 厌恶 / 喜欢。注意第二个人的用词——他 / 她往往先肯定或否定第一个人，常用 right / but / really（升调），再进一步说明。其次要确认代词是 it / he / she / they，弄清考查的是男士、女士，还是双方共同的态度。

题型 5 · 行为去向

行为与去向题

Action & Intention
真题复现
A. Feed the dog. B. Clean the dog's house. C. Give the dog a bath. D. Find something to eat.
听力原文

M: Before feeding the dog, I'm going to clear up its house.

W: It's about time. Look at the mess! It smells terrible.

Q: What is the man most probably going to do first?

▸ 答案 + 解析
B（Clean the dog's house）
男士开头说的 before 是解题关键：before feeding the dog（喂狗之前），他要先 clear up its house（清理狗窝）；女士的回答 It's about time 是对男士的肯定。
技巧点拨

注意说话人谈及过去、现在与未来的行动。谈话中常涉及多个行动，重点应放在所做的事及对谈话者的影响，判断说话者想干什么、不想干什么，从而做出选择。before / after / first / then 等先后顺序词是关键线索。

题型 6 · 推断意图

推断与意图题

Inference
真题复现
A. She wished she had gone to sleep earlier. B. She missed the beginning of the program. C. She fell asleep before the program ended. D. She was awakened in time to see the program.
听力原文

M: I really enjoyed that TV special about dolphins last night, did you get home in time to see it?

W: Well, yes. But I wish I could have stayed awake long enough to see the whole thing.

Question: What does the woman mean?

▸ 答案 + 解析
C（She fell asleep before the program ended）
别被开头的 Well, yes 蒙蔽。真正的信号在弱读的 but 之后：虚拟语气 I wish I could have stayed awake（真希望自己一直醒着），说明女士没有看完就睡着了。
技巧点拨

对话中没有直接给答案，要拿细节做前提进行逻辑推理或归纳。题目常是 What can be inferred from the conversation? / What does the man/woman imply?。此时要加倍注意第二个人的话——这类题多是「第一个人开启话题 → 第二个人提供信息 → 提问」的模式，逻辑关系多在第二个人话里。还要注意虚拟语气、升降调找线索。

记叙文 · 真题（司机）

Narrative
真题复现Questions 11–13 based on the following passage
11. A. He regards it as an ideal job and is afraid of losing it.     B. He likes this job and does it professionally.     C. It is a job looked down upon by other people.     D. 技巧点拨 · 记叙文

记叙文以记人、叙事为主（幽默故事、童话、历史、传记、新闻），语言以一般过去时为主，多用动态动词和直接引语。听时要把握三点：① 结构（时间 / 空间顺序，理清脉络）；② 写作目的（就事论事还是讲道理）；③ 六要素（事件、人物、时间、地点、原因、结果）。

Part 3 · 说明文

说明文 · 真题（窄船）

Expository
真题复现Questions 11–13 based on the following passage
11. A. Most canals were not wide enough for the boats.     B. Other means of transportation became accessible.     C. The boats were no longer considered fashionable.     D. They learned the boats were bad for the environment. 12. A. Some people get frustrated with their speed.     B. They are mainly used for transportation.     C. People can have easy access to them.     D. A license is needed to operate them. 13. A. The changing role of narrow boats.     B. The uniqueness of the design of narrow boats.     C. The importance of narrow boats in the 18th century.     D. The reason why British people say hello to strangers.
听力原文

Do you know anything about narrow boats in Britain? The boats are those you see in the British canals which are truly narrow, about two metres wide. They are, however, long enough to have bedrooms, kitchens and bathrooms, where people can live for weeks at a time. These days, more and more people are spending their vacations on narrow boats. We can trace the origin of the boats to the beginning of the Industrial Revolution. In the mid eighteenth century, British people constructed many canals all over the island of Great Britain. The canals were narrow, and the boats had to be narrow too. The boats were used to transport coal, iron and steel. After railroads became common, narrow boats went out of fashion and were forgotten, only the canals remained. Why are the boats so popular today? First, as there are canals all over Britain, narrow boats can be easily accessed. People do not have to travel far or rush to an airport to start their vacations. Second, the boats are safe and easy to steer. Even though they have engines now, they sail just a little faster than walking speed, so no license is required. Finally, the slow pace makes the voyage fun. People on board enjoy saying hello to other boaters when they pass each other. Since most of the canals are for leisure use, nobody is in a hurry. In this way, British people can refresh themselves and forget about their busy life. What used to carry iron and steel now provides a slow, peaceful experience.

Part 3 · 说明文

说明文 · 解析与技巧

Expository Analysis
答案 + 解析
▸ 11. Why did people once stop using narrow boats?
B
听到 After railroads became common, narrow boats went out of fashion...，可知是铁路改变了船的作用；原文的「铁路」被替换为「上位概念」其他交通工具。
▸ 12. What is true of modern narrow boats?
C
听到 narrow boats can be easily accessed，对应选项 C 的 have easy access to（同义替换）。
▸ 13. What is the passage mainly about?
A
文章按说明顺序：先讲船的现在作用→ 再讲历史（以前运货）→ 最后讲现在的好处，主旨是「窄船角色的变化」。
技巧点拨 · 说明文

说明文介绍事物的特征、本质、性能、状态、用途、原理。语言特征：① 多用现在时态；② 客观描述，多用被动语态（It is said that... 替代 I think that...）、权威引用（according to sb. 替代 in my opinion）；③ 遣词简练平实，不加议论。听时要弄清说明顺序：时间、空间、逻辑（因果）、认识顺序（由浅入深、由现象到本质）。

14. A. It can increase the rate of learning and the likelihood of remembering.     B. It can help gather related information.     C. The information can be easily recalled during an actual debate.     D. It makes remembering information difficult. 15. A. Reading and thinking alone.     B. Talking about a movie to friends.     C. Writing down what they've read.     D. Read the information loudly. 16. A. Related information is put together logically.     B. All the information is put together.     C. Information is related and can be recalled.     D. You can recall any information anytime.
听力原文

Some people believe that spending quiet hours reading and thinking in a library is the best way to prepare for a debate contest. Most memory experts, however, would recommend preparing with team members. Doing so increases the rate of learning and the likelihood of remembering. When members of a debate team prepare together, they learn facts and master issues faster and more effectively than each individual could alone.

"Communication is essential to the ability to remember well," says a well-known psychologist. He has found that isolating yourself is about the worst thing you can do if you want to remember information. In other words, when you talk with others, whether it is to describe a movie or complain about a difficult chapter in a textbook, you are actually strengthening your memory. The human memory works like a filing system. For a filing system to be effective, information has to be put in a logical place inside a folder that includes related information.

When the members of a debate team practise by using facts in support of an argument, they are exactly gathering related pieces of information into a single mental folder. And during an actual debate, when the pressure is on, they will be more easily recalled than those memorized as isolated pieces of information.

Part 3 · 议论文

议论文 · 解析

Argumentative Analysis
答案 + 解析
▸ 14. What do memory experts think of reading quietly in debate preparation?
D
抓住 however：专家不认同默默记忆。讨论可加强记忆，反之默默记忆使记忆变难，故 D。
▸ 15. In which situation are people most likely to improve memory?
B
听到 when you talk with others... describe a movie or complain about a difficult chapter...，结合主旨「交流方便记忆」，选 B（向朋友谈论电影）。
▸ 16. In what way does human memory work like a filing system?
A
选项接近，抓住不同点：A「related, logically」、B「All, together」、C「related, can be recalled」、D「recall, anytime」。原文 information has to be put in a logical place inside a folder that includes related information → A。
🔑
议论文三要素：论点（作者观点）· 论据（证明论点的依据）· 论证（用论据证明论点的过程）。结构上一般由序论（what）→ 本论（why）→ 结论（how）三部分构成。
Part 3 · 议论文

议论文 · 三种结构模式

Argument Patterns
模式一 · 层层递进

引言段（提出观点）→ 正方论点 1 → 正方论点 2 → 正方论点 3 → 结论段。论点由弱到强排列，最强论点放最后以示强调。

模式二 · 先驳后立

引言段（提出观点）→ 反面意见（反方 + 作者反驳）→ 正方论点 1 / 2 / 3 → 结论段。开头先驳对立观点，再陈述己方论点。

模式三 · 边驳边立

引言段 → 反方观点 1 + 反驳、反方观点 2 + 反驳、反方观点 3 + 反驳 → 结论段。每一部分都先抛反方观点再反驳。

议论文语言特点 · 演讲文体

议论文语言客观、抽象、概括，准确且合乎逻辑，多用 since / now that / therefore / in that case / so / It follows that... / even if... 等辩论、推理类连接词。演讲文体（speech / talk / lecture）近年高频：先称呼语（ladies and gentlemen / fellow students），再开门见山提出论题（最生动的是举例法），接着客观论证（举例 / 因果 / 对比），最后得出结论。

Part 4 · 长对话

长对话 · 题型概述

Long Conversations

长对话由一段或两段较长对话组成，通常读两遍。涉及范围广：求职面试、个人履历、预订房间、饭店订座、预订机票、预约演出等日常小事，考查考生对细节的反馈和辨音能力。

1

听前浏览选项

预测主题与可能听到的词汇；把关键词、短语画线，联想其同义词、近义词。
2

听中做笔记

在选项旁记人名、地名、数据、事实，用关键词或短语做简要提纲，减轻记忆负担。
3

做完要复盘

不仅看是否答对，还要弄懂其他选项为何错，并与录音原文比对。
💡
选项性质判定：答案选项可能是直接 / 间接支持（正确），直接 / 间接矛盾（错误），或根本未提及（错误）。
Part 4 · 长对话

错误答案 & 正确答案的判定

Judging Answers
✗

错误答案的三种情况

① 逻辑不符：选项与录音内容逻辑不符或没有意义。
② 直接矛盾：与录音内容完全相反（或几乎一样但非 100% 相同）。
③ 间接矛盾 / 未提及：听出某些信息使该选项不正确，或录音根本没提。
✓

正确答案的两种情况

① 直接支持：录音内容直接支持该答案。
② 间接支持：某部分录音让你觉得它正确；常用同义词 / 类似表达改写，或在说话者的表单、摘要、定义中找到。
典型例题
A. couples with children B. people who are physically fit C. single people D. retired people
听力原文

This is a great tour for young, professional people who want to get away from the rat race for a while and do something totally different. We usually only accept people who are mid-30s or younger. It's not really suitable for families, though, as there's quite a bit of climbing involved and we walk or ride bikes for several hours each day. You should be in shape and ready for a bit of a challenge physically. It doesn't really matter if you're part of a couple or not. We can arrange for you to share a room.

Question: Which group of people does the travel agent think the tour is most suitable for?

▸ 答案 + 解析
B（people who are physically fit）
① D「retired people」与「30 岁左右、年轻」不符；② A「couples with children」与「不适合家庭」直接矛盾；③ C「single people」是间接矛盾——「不携伴也可以」说明单身、有伴都可，并非「最适合单身者」。而 be in shape / challenge physically 直接支持 B。
Part 4 · 长对话

长对话 · 三大易错点

Common Mistakes
①

过度预测

看了选项就以为知道答案、不再细听；或一听到「疑似」对的答案就停止注意后续内容。
②

没读全选项

务必读完所有选项，尤其选项较长时；注意答案关键词常用同义词替代。
③

漏听修饰词

注意 only / always / never / un- / over / under / before / after / non- 等会限制范围的词，及 women / men 替代 people 的情况。
典型例题
A. He likes to do a lot of exercises when he is on vacation. B. He is a young professional under the age of 35. C. He doesn't have any children. D. He seldom goes rock climbing.
听力原文

This is a great tour for young, professional people who want to get away from the rat race for a while and do something totally different. We usually only accept people who are mid-30s or younger. It's not really suitable for families, though, as there's quite a bit of climbing involved and we walk or ride bikes for several hours each day. You should be in shape and ready for a bit of a challenge physically. It doesn't really matter if you're part of a couple or not. We can arrange for you to share a room.

Ian: Hmmm... Well, I've never tried rock climbing before but it sounds fun and it'll be good to keep fit while on holidays. The main advantage for me is that I'll be with people like me. I don't want to get stuck on a tour with a group of retired people. What would we talk about?

Question: Why does Ian decide to go on the 14-day adventure tour of New Zealand?

▸ 答案 + 解析
B（He is a young professional under the age of 35）
录音虽没有直接说出，但 Ian 自己（with people like me）和旅行社代理的叙述（mid-30s or younger）都表明他属于「35 岁以下的年轻职业人士」——这是间接支持的答案。
Part 4 · 长对话

长对话 · 综合例题（Laura）

Practice
典型例题Questions 17–20
17. A. Her work life is very busy.     B. She needs to be in a different environment.     C. She wants to go shopping.     D. She is available recently. 18. A. a warm place.     B. a place with snow.     C. a new place.     D. a humid environment. 19. A. 5 weeks     B. 4 weeks     C. 3 weeks     D. 2 weeks 20. A. alone     B. with one or two friends     C. with a relative     D. in a group
听力原文

M: Good morning, Laura. How are you?

W: I'm well, thanks. Thank you for meeting with me on such short notice. I just really need a total change of scene!

M: It's easy to get stuck in a rut, isn't it? A holiday can work wonders. Did you have any ideas of where you'd like to go and what style of holiday you'd like?

W: Well, I was thinking of an island holiday or maybe somewhere in Asia. Sometimes it's nice to go somewhere warm. I don't want to do a skiing holiday. I think the main point is that I go somewhere I haven't been before.

M: That can be true but it also depends on the tour. Do you know how long you'd like to go for?

W: Ideally, I'd take a month but I think 14 days is more realistic. At least I don't have to worry about coordinating with friends and relatives since I'm going by myself.

M: I can easily put something together for that timeframe. Oh, we have a new package to the Cook Islands.

W: Well, I've heard that the Cook Islands are beautiful. I think I'd like to go there. Thanks a lot.

🎧
先作答 17 / 18 / 19 / 20，再看下一页答案 + 解析。
Part 4 · 长对话

长对话 · Laura 解析

Analysis
答案 + 解析
▸ 17. Why does Laura need a holiday?
B
细节理解：Laura 说 I just really need a total change of scene!（我需要换个完全不同的景色）。
▸ 18. Where is Laura thinking of going?
C
细节区别：虽想去「somewhere warm」、不喜欢滑雪，但关键在 the main point is that I go somewhere I haven't been before（去从没去过的地方）。
▸ 19. How long will Laura most likely go on holiday?
D
细节区别：虽「a month」最理想，但她认为 14 days is more realistic（14 天更实际）→ 2 weeks。
▸ 20. How will Laura go on holiday?
A
细节理解：她说不用协调亲戚朋友的时间，因为 I'm going by myself（独自前往）。
🎯
解析共性：长对话每题基本都考「细节 + 同义替换」——答案选项往往不是原文原句，而是换个说法（change of scene → different environment；14 days → 2 weeks；by myself → alone）。
