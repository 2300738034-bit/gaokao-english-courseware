一 · 问答与否定

考点1 · 不用 yes / no 回答的疑问句

No Yes/No Answers
① 回答「是否介意」
— Would you mind my turning off the radio?
— Of course not. / Not at all.
② 接受邀请 / 提议
— Will you please send her a message?
— I'm glad to.
③ 主观判断
— Is it worth reading?
— I think so.
💡
口语/听力高频：Would you mind… 的否定回答是「不介意」（Of course not），肯定回答才表示「介意」（I'm sorry, but I would）。别搞反。
一 · 问答与否定

考点2 · not…because 的否定转移

Transferred Negation
否定转移（否定 because 从句）
The mountain is not famous because it is high.
这座山出名并不是因为高（否定的是原因，不是「出名」）。
不发生转移（否定主句）
We did not go camping because it was raining hard.
因为雨大，所以我们没去野营（否定的是主句动作）。
⚠️
歧义处理：「The company didn't fire him because he was elderly.」有两种译法。为避免歧义：否定主句时，把 because 从句提前或加逗号；否定从句时用 not because…but because 或强调句 It was not because…that…。
▸ 翻译练习（真题风格）
It was not because he was late that he was criticized.
正因为他面试迟到，才被批评。（用强调句锁定否定对象）
一 · 问答与否定

考点3 · 否定的「虚实移位」

Shifted Negation

形式否定前半部分、实际否定后半部分，容易理解相反。核心是 not A and B（否定 and 后面的 B）和 not some（部分否定）。

not A and B（否定 B）
Don't drink and drive.（喝酒就别开车）
The motel is not big and cozy.（虽大但不舒服）
not + 不定式（not 前置）
Our monitor didn't happen to be there.（碰巧不在）
My friend did not pretend to see me.（假装没看见）
not some（部分否定）
I don't like some of the paintings.（有些不喜欢）
对比：don't like any = 全部否定（一幅都不喜欢）。
▸ 翻译练习
He is a clever boy, but not hard-working.
他是个聪明的孩子，但不勤奋。（not…and 部分否定）
一 · 问答与否定

考点4 · 形式否定、意义肯定

Negative Form, Positive Meaning
① cannot…too…（再…也不为过）
I cannot thank you too much.
= 无论怎么感谢都不为过。可用 can hardly/never + too/over/enough 替换。
② 否定词 + 比较级（= 最高级）
I can't agree more.（非常同意）
There is nothing Tom likes more than beer.
③ cannot but / cannot help doing（不得不/禁不住）
We cannot but learn to improve ourselves.
Everybody can't help admiring his wisdom.
④ no…but（没有…不）
There is no rule but has exceptions.
There is nobody but knows it.
✍️
写作加分：I can't agree more.（我非常赞同）、A person can't be too careful.（越谨慎越好）、You can never praise a child too much.（表扬越多越好）—— 议论文里表达强烈态度的高分句。
一 · 问答与否定

考点5 · 形式肯定、意义否定

Positive Form, Negative Meaning
more than…can（不可能/难以）
I love you more than I can say.
It was more than his mother could bear.
anything but（根本不是）
It was anything but safe.（很不安全）
He is anything but a scholar.
比较级 + than + to do（不至于）
The boy is older than to ask a stranger for candies.
I know better than to go out alone.
✍️
写作加分：…more than I can express / say（难以言表）、be anything but + adj.（绝非……）是表达强烈情感的正式句式，读后续写、议论文结尾都适用。
二 · 比较结构

考点6 · 「the more…, the more…」的变化

The more…the more
基本结构 + 条件后置
The more you eat, the fatter you'll get.
You'll get (the) fatter, the more you eat.
主句在前时，比较级前 the 可省略。
名词前必须带 the
The higher the mountain, the greener the pines.
不能说 The higher mountain, the greener pines。
✍️
写作加分：The more…, the more… 是作文里最省力又拿分的结构——「The more we read, the wiser we become.」「The harder you work, the better you'll get.」开头、论证、结尾都能用。
三 · 被动语态

考点7-8 · 双重被动 & 不能变被动的结构

Double Passive
考点7 · 双重被动（动词+sb.+to do sth.）
The project was asked to be redesigned at once.
Books of this kind are not intended to be read by children.
「请求/禁止某人做某事」转被动有三种，其中双重被动最常见于正式书面语。
考点8 · 不能变被动的结构
反身代词/相互代词：She dresses herself.
同源宾语：Tom smiled a forced smile.
将来进行时/完成进行时、had better/would rather、固定词组（make up one's mind、take office 等）也无被动。
💡
识别点：看到 be + 过去分词 + to be + 过去分词（was asked to be redesigned）就是双重被动，翻译成「被要求/被希望去被……」，多用于新闻报道和正式文体。
四 · 定语从句

考点9-10 · 状语含义 & 关系代词省略

Relative Clauses
考点9 · 定语从句的状语含义
John, who was ready to take every opportunity, found a well-paid job.（因果）
A rescue measure was taken, which brought financial benefits.（结果）
翻译时按时间/条件/因果/目的/让步/结果补连词。
考点10 · 关系代词省略
Is he the person (whom) you are looking for?
That is the man (who) discovered the cause.
作宾语可省；在 there be 从句作主语、That is / It is / There is / Here is + 先行词结构中，主语 that/who 也可省略。
✍️
写作加分：用非限制性定语从句表结果（…, which made/led to/brought…）是续写和议论文里的高级衔接，比两个短句更流畅。
四 · 定语从句

考点11-12 · there be 定语从句 & that 让步

More Relative Clauses
考点11 · there be 引导限制性定语从句
He has met every difficulty (that) there is.
This is the only chance (that) there is.
意为「目前有的/曾经有过的」，that/who 作主语常省略。
考点12 · that 表让步/评论/感叹（倒装）
Uneducated that I am, I know what's good for me.
Hero that you are, you saved the child!
表语提前 + that 引导，相当于 as/though 让步或感叹语气。
▸ 翻译练习
He is the person who claimed to have experienced it.
他就是那个声称经历过此事的人。（That is + 先行词 + 定语从句）
五 · 条件与让步

考点13 · 条件概念的其他表达

Other Conditionals
祈使句 + and/or
Come here, and you will get what you want.
Conquer your desires, or they will conquer you.
but for / but that（要不是）
But for your help, I could not have succeeded.
But that Tom arrived in time, she might have lost her child.
must…before…（只有…才…）
One must sow before one can reap.
Where there is a will, there is a way.
✍️
写作加分：祈使句 + and/or（Believe in yourself, and you will succeed.）、Without…（Without a cool mind, we can't decide well.）都是议论文万能句。
五 · 条件与让步

考点14 · 让步概念的其他表达

Other Concessions
It is true that…but…
It is true that he is a kid, but he respects others.
Be + 主语 + ever so + 形容词
Be a man ever so rich, he should serve others.
动词 + what/when/where + may
Come when you will, you are always welcome.
Be the consequences what they may, we'll move on.
▸ 翻译练习
Young as he is, he knows how to deal with different people.
虽然他很年轻，但知道如何与不同的人打交道。（as 让步倒装）
六 · 不定式与动名词

考点15-16 · come to do & to have done

come to do
考点15 · come to do（逐渐）
She had come to love him.
Five years later, Peter came to be an artist.
表「逐渐」的过程，get to do 同义。
考点16 · to have done 作主语（虚拟）
To have been told the truth would have given me another chance.
表「假如过去…」，谓语用 would/should + 完成时。
▸ 翻译练习
After years of effort, he came to be an engineer.
多年努力之后，他成为一名工程师。（come to do 表渐进）
六 · 不定式与动名词

考点17 · 接不定式 / 动名词含义不同

to do vs doing
remember / forget / regret
remember to do（记得要做）/doing（记得做过）
forget to do / doing · regret to do / doing
try / stop / mean
try to do（尽力）/ doing（尝试）
stop to do（停下做另一件事）/ doing（停止正在做）
mean to do（打算）/ doing（意味着）
want / need / deserve
want to do（想）/ doing（需要被做）
deserve to do（值得）/ doing（该被）
▸ 真题回放（2022 秋考）
Mom forgot buying cucumbers for our neighbor two days ago.
妈妈忘了两天前刚给邻居买过黄瓜。（forget doing = 忘了做过）
六 · 不定式与动名词

考点18-19 · 名词化动名词

Nominal Gerunds
考点18 · 有复数的名词化动名词
Everyone has failings, so don't feel disappointed.
The survey's findings are a matter of concern.
comings and goings、doings、sufferings、burnings、cryings 等有复数形式。
考点19 · 以动名词形式出现的名词
belongings（随身物品）· earnings（收入）· surroundings（环境）· winnings（奖金）
注意：有同根名词时用同根名词（management 不用 managing，refusal 不用 refusing）。
▸ 翻译练习
He described the sufferings his race had gone through in many novels.
他在很多小说中描述了他的种族所遭受的种种苦难。（suffering 名词化复数）
七 · 分词

考点20 · 无逻辑主语的分词悬垂结构

Dangling Participles
单个分词（已成习惯用语）
Considering his health, we asked him to stop.
Regarding your request, she said nothing.
considering / including / regarding / concerning / barring / wanting 等。
已转介词短语
Judging from his look, he is a teacher.
Owing to the rain, the match was put off.
副词 + speaking
Frankly speaking, I'm not keen on it.
Strictly speaking, that's not a dictionary.
✍️
写作加分：Generally/Frankly/Strictly speaking 是作文开头的万能衔接；Judging from…、Considering… 让论证更有层次。
八 · 虚拟语气

考点21-22 · 虚拟语气的特殊结构

Special Subjunctive
It is + adj. + that…（should）do
It is necessary/vital/essential that we (should) take action.
necessary、vital、essential、desirable、urgent 等后接虚拟。
for fear that / lest / in order that
He kept silent for fear that he would disturb the teacher.
Father repeated it lest my mother (should) forget it.
would rather + 从句（过去时/完成时）
I'd rather you had a chance to visit now.
We'd sooner she had passed the test last time.
Be it…or / 祝愿句
Be it cheap or not, I will get it.
God bless you!（bless 不加 s）
✍️
写作加分：It is essential that we (should)… 是建议类作文的高分句；I'd rather… 表达愿望。
九 · 倒装与反意

考点23 · 其他倒装结构

More Inversion
① 副词/介词 + with + 名词（命令）
In with you, boys!
Up with the flowers, Tom!
② 行为动词 + 主语 + 情态/助动词（强调）
It's my duty to help you, and do it I will.
③ of 短语 / ever / never 前置
Of the thirty students, ten passed.
We never have and never will retreat.
④ 比较/方式状语从句倒装
Mothers give more than will fathers.
He is honest, as are most high-achievers.
💡
注意：本节是拔高拓展，看懂即可；但 ④ 的 as 引导的倒装（as do/are…）在阅读和写作里很实用。
九 · 倒装与反意

考点24 · 反意疑问句的几种特殊情况

Tag Questions
含 only → 肯/否均可
There were only five people, weren't/were they?
主语 A+B → 视情况
You and I designed it, didn't we?
Whisky and soda is popular, isn't it?
It is the first time…
It is the first time you've visited, isn't it?
It is high time we left, isn't it?
⚠️
对比：含 only 的主句，反意疑问部分肯定否定都行；但 It is the first time… 只能用 isn't it（不随从句走）。
十 · 强调

考点25-30 · 强调手段（一）

Emphasis 1
重复名词/动词（很多/种类）
There are books and books in his basement.
She thought and thought.
the last + 名词（最不可能）
Tom is the last person to blame.
shall 表命令/规定
You shall be there at 8:00.
Children under 10 shall see it with parents.
at all / on earth / in the world
Where on earth did you see it?
Do you know the truth at all?
nothing but / anything but
He is nothing but a novelist.（只是）
He is anything but stupid.（绝不）
十 · 强调

考点31-34 · 强调手段（二）

Emphasis 2
形容词后置
Such is Einstein, great and humorous.
Everybody, male or female, should obey.
as…as 加强语气
I have as many as 100 supporters.
His explanation is as clear as clear can be.
do / never do 强调动词
Please do come.
The real reason never did come to light.
none of（绝不是）
It's none of your business.
Tom is none of my friends.
✍️
写作加分：do + 动词原形（I do believe…）、形容词后置（…, beautiful and touching）、as…as 强调都是让句子出彩的手段。
十一 · the way

考点35 · the way 的四重用法

The Way
① 名词（= the manner 方式）
The way he speaks is impressive.
② 副词（= how 如何）
That is the way we did the job.
③ 连词（= as 像 / if 如果）
Tom dealt with others the way his mother did.
The way you are working, you'll fail.
④ in the way（以某种方式）
They cooked chicken in the way I appreciated.
注意「in the way + 句子」中 in 不可省略。
▸ 翻译练习
We will love and serve our country the way our fathers did.
我们将像父辈那样热爱并服务于国家。（the way = as）
十二 · 真题精练

高考真题回放（上海卷）

Real Gaokao
翻译 · 2022 秋考
妈妈忘了两天前刚给邻居买过黄瓜。（forget）
▸ 答案
Mom forgot buying cucumbers for our neighbor two days ago.
考点：forget doing（忘了做过）。
翻译 · 2022 秋考
这条运河历经数百年才修建而成，如今虽无昔日繁荣之景，但仍然是横跨东西的重要水路。（as…as）
▸ 答案
The canal took hundreds of years to build, and though it is no longer as prosperous as before, it is still an important waterway running across the east and the west.
考点：as…as 比较 + 让步。
翻译 · 2023 秋考
正如预期的那样，艺术节引起了学生对于民俗文化的兴趣。（generate）
▸ 答案
As was expected, the art festival generated students' interest in folk culture.
考点：As was expected 定语从句。
翻译 · 2023 秋考
你是否意识到这个地区的每一个部落有自己的不成文的规则？（aware）
▸ 答案
Are you aware that every tribe in this region has its own unwritten rules?
考点：be aware that + 宾语从句 + 主谓一致（every tribe → has）。
💡
实战提醒：上海卷翻译题的括号提示词是定位考点的钥匙——forget→动名词、so that→目的、as…as→比较、where→定语从句、aware→宾语从句。先锁定提示词对应的结构，再组织句子。

本章小结

Summary

否定（形式 ↔ 意义）

  • not…because：否定转移 / 不转移。
  • not A and B：否定 B；not some：部分否定。
  • cannot…too / 否定词+比较级：意义肯定。
  • more than…can / anything but：意义否定。

强调（十种手段）

  • 重复名词/动词、the last、shall。
  • at all / on earth / nothing but / anything but。
  • 形容词后置 / as…as / do+原形 / none of。

非谓语 & 虚拟

  • come to do（逐渐）、to have done（虚拟主语）。
  • remember/forget/stop/mean + to do / doing。
  • 名词化动名词（有复数）。
  • It is essential that…(should)、lest/for fear that。

从句 & 其他

  • 定语从句状语化（因果/结果/让步）。
  • there be 定语从句、that 让步倒装。
  • 条件的其他表达（祈使句+and/or、but for）。
  • the way（名词/副词/连词）。
翻译题先看「括号提示词」锁定考点 · 写作里主动用「cannot too / the more / 定语从句状语化 / do 强调」—— 考点在手，翻译写作都加分
