一 · 问答与否定

考点1 · 不用 yes / no 回答的疑问句

No Yes/No Answers

一般疑问句通常要求作肯定/否定回答，但以下几种情况不需要用 yes/no。

1. 回答「是否介意」
— Would you mind my turning off the radio now？你介意我关掉收音机吗？
— Of course not. / Not at all. / Certainly not. 不介意，请关。
— I'm sorry, but I would. 不好意思，请不要关。
2. 接受邀请 / 提议，或致歉
— Shall we go to the seaside this weekend？周末去海边好吗？
— That's a good idea, but you see I am too busy.
— Will you please send her a message for me？ — I'm glad to.
— Can I keep the book for a few days？ — Sorry, but you have to return it tomorrow.
3. 回答带有责备意味的句子
— Do you remember what you promised me to do？记得答应过我什么吗？
— I am sorry, sir.
4. 回答含主观判断的问题
— Is it worth reading？ — I think so.
— Are the trousers too short？ — I think they are all right.
5. 对选择疑问句的回答
— Shall we see her in the morning or in the evening？
— I prefer in the morning.
💡
口语/听力高频：Would you mind… 的否定回答是「不介意」（Of course not.），肯定回答才表示「介意」。
一 · 问答与否定

考点2 · 主句含有 not 的 because 从句

Transferred Negation
1. 否定转移（not 实则否定 because 从句）
The mountain is not famous because it is high.这座山出名并不是因为它高。
The machine did not stop because there was no fuel.机器停并不是因为没油。
We play basketball not because the teacher asks us to but because we want to.不是老师要我们打，而是我们想打。
2. 不发生否定转移（not 否定主句动词）
We did not go camping because it was raining hard.因雨大，没去野营。
I did not lend money to him because I was hard up myself.因自己拮据，没借钱给他。
3. 两种情况皆可（The company didn't fire him because he was elderly.）
Because he was elderly, the company didn't fire him.因他年纪大，公司没解雇他。
It was not because he was elderly that the company fired him.公司解雇他，不是因为他年纪大。
避免歧义：否定主句时把 because 从句提前或加逗号；否定从句时用 not because 或强调句 It is/was…that…。
📌
只能用 because 的场合：not…but…结构、强调句、原因从句作表语、有副词修饰状语从句时——都不能用 for/since/as 替代。如 That is because he is wise. / Father lost his temper just because the son failed twice.

  2. 我之所以选择他不是因为他毕业于一所著名大学，而是因为他交际能力强。not because
  3. 仅仅是因为他未被大学录取，你就对他失去了信心？simply because
  4. —他们为什么相处得很好？It
    —那是因为他们彼此之间有了进一步的了解。
▸参考答案

一 · 问答与否定

考点3 · 否定的「虚实移位」

Shifted Negation

形式上否定前半部分、实际上否定后半部分。核心结构：not A and B、not + 不定式、not some。

1. not A and B（否定 and 后的 B）
Don't drink and drive.酒后不开车，开车不喝酒。
The motel is not big and cozy.虽大，但不舒服。
He is not rich and kind.他为富不仁。
He does not speak Chinese and German.说汉语，不说德语。
We cannot put on airs and make people like us.摆架子，别人就不喜欢我们。
2. happen / prove / pretend + 不定式被否定时 not 前置
Our monitor didn't happen to be there.碰巧班长不在。
My friend did not pretend to see me.假装没看见我。
This did not prove to be a problem.证明这不是问题。
3. not some（部分否定）
I don't like some of the paintings.有些画不喜欢。
I won't invite some of them.有些人就不邀请了。
比较：I don't like any of the paintings.（一幅都不喜欢）
全部否定 vs 部分否定
I don't know anything about him.（全部否定）
I don't know everything about him.（部分否定）
All the answers are not right.（部分否定）
None of the answers is right.（全部否定）
前后两部分都用否定时，可用 neither…nor… 或用 or 连接：He is neither active nor creative. / There is no air or water on the moon.
翻译练习 2 （考点3）

  2. 她说得正确但不够清楚，所以她还得努力提高一下技巧。not…and
  3. 我看着陈列柜，有些物品我不喜欢。not some
  4. 由于时间不够，这些书我不可能全看。not some
  5. 这匹马不能既驮你的行李又驮他的行李，它毕竟是匹小马。not…and
  6. 当听到声响的时候，这个小男孩假装没在打游戏。pretend
  7. 我的班长假装不知道该项目，把机会留给了其他人。pretend
  8. 他碰巧当时不在邮局，否则的话，他就会帮我捎带几张纪念邮票了。happen
▸参考答案
  1. He is a clever boy, but not diligent.
  2. What she said was right but not clear enough, so she has to improve her skills.
  3. Looking at the display case, I don't like some of the items.
  4. As time is short, I can't read all of these books / I can't read some of these books.
  5. The horse can't carry both your luggage and his luggage; after all, it is a pony.
  6. Hearing the sound, the boy pretended not to be playing games.
  7. My monitor pretended not to know about the project, leaving the chance to others.
  8. He happened not to be at the post office then; otherwise he would have brought me some commemorative stamps.

考点4 · 形式否定、意义肯定

Negative Form, Positive Meaning
1. cannot…too / over / enough（再…也不为过）
I cannot thank you too much.
A person can't be too cautious.
A kid can't see his mother soon enough.
A teacher can never praise a student's progress too much.
2. 否定词 + 比较级（= 最高级）
I can't agree more.非常同意。
I couldn't feel better.好极了。
There is nothing Tom likes more than beer.
Nobody is more brutal than a dictator.
You can't find a better motel than mine here.
3. 不得不 / 禁不住
We cannot but learn to improve ourselves.
I can't help but feel saddened for his failure.
Everybody can't help admiring his wisdom.
4. but 引导定语从句（没有…不）
There is nobody but knows it.
There is no rule but has exceptions.
Nobody sees the film but will be moved.
No materials exist but deform under force.
5. more often than not（常常）
He wins more often than not in competitions.
6. 否定感叹句（= 肯定感叹）
Isn't he brave and generous！
What bad deeds would he not do！
7. not a little / not a bit 等
not a little = 很 · not a bit = 一点也不
no little = much 很多no few = many 许多 no small = great 很大quite a little = much 相当多 quite a few = many 相当多
The young artist is not a little experienced.
My brother spent no small amount on sneakers.
Not once or twice have I told him to give up smoking.

  3. 一个人的世界观必然会在他的言行中表现出来。cannot but
  4. 没有人一生不犯错误的。no…but
  5. 那条大街经常拥挤，我还是避开吧。more often than not

  9. 他有很多书，其中有些都没有读过。not a few

一 · 问答与否定

考点5(上) · 形式肯定、意义否定（1–6）

Positive Form, Negative Meaning
1. more than…can（不可能 / 难以）
I love you more than I can say.爱你在心口难开。
This is more than I can tell.这件事我不能讲。
It was more than his mother could bear.
2. more A than B（与其说 B 不如说 A）
Zhang Fei-like heroes are more brave than wise.有勇无谋。
I left because I was more annoyed than frightened.
It is more a dictionary than a grammar book.
3. 比较级 + than + to do（不至于 / 懂得不该）
The boy is older than to ask a stranger for candies.
Each of us is wiser than to believe his words.
I know better than to go out alone.
4. anything but（根本不是）
The wooden bridge was anything but safe.
He is anything but a scholar.
5. might / may as well（最好 / 不如）
You might as well buy the tickets right now.最好现在买（建议）。
We might as well stay indoors.不如待在室内（否定）。
You may as well throw the money into the sea as lend it to him.
6. It is…since…（瞬间动词→肯定 / 延续动词→否定）
It is five years since he started to work here.工作已五年（瞬间→肯定）。
It is five years since he worked here.五年不在此工作（延续→否定）。
It has been two years since he fell ill.病了两年。
It has been two years since he was ill.病好两年了。

二 · 比较结构

考点6 · 「the more…, the more…」的变化

The more…the more
1. 条件后置（主句在前，the 可省）
The more you eat, the fatter you'll get.
You'll get (the) fatter, the more you eat.
The more learned a man is, the more modest he usually is.
→ The man is usually (the) more modest, the more learned he is.
The result is better, the harder you practise.
2. 名词前必须有 the
The higher the mountain, the greener the pines.
不能说 The higher mountain, the greener pines.
The bigger the apples, the bigger the sales.
The higher the price, the better the quality.
The nearer the dawn, the darker the night.
The higher the mountain, the purer the air.
同义By how much the higher you climb, by so much the more scenery you will see.（登得越高，看到的风景越多）
To what extent you study harder, to that extent you get more.（学习越努力，获得越多）
翻译练习 5 （考点6）
  1. 单词记得越多，你就会发现英语越容易学。
  2. 树越多，环境越好。
  3. 在岛上逗留的时间越长，你越舍不得回家。
  4. 越接近黎明，夜晚就越黑暗。
  5. 越多越好。
  6. 走得有多快，你就会变得多敏捷。
  7. 你在多大程度上积极主动，就有多大可能获得这个职位。

三 · 被动语态

考点7 · 被动语态的转移与双重被动

Double Passive
They asked us to redesign the project at once. → 三种被动
(1) We were asked to redesign the project.
(2) They asked the project to be redesigned.
(3) The project was asked to be redesigned.（双重被动）
「动词 + sb. + to do sth.」（请求/禁止某人做）转被动：① 把 sb. 变主语；② 把 sth. 变主语；③ 把 sth. 变宾语后接不定式被动式——即双重被动。
更多双重被动对照
The headmaster persuaded the program to be supported by the parents.
→ The program was persuaded to be supported.
The desk is not meant to be stood on by the boy.
Books of this kind are not intended to be read by children.
第二个被动式可直接用过去分词
All the shops are ordered closed.
They were found surrounded by wild animals.
The cave was reported found by farmers last week.
① 主语+谓语+不定式+宾语
A computer was offered to be bought for me.
② 主语+谓语+that 从句
The event is thought to have been experienced by my family.
③ 主语+谓语+宾语+不定式被动式
The tree is wanted to be moved to Mr. Lee.

考点17 · 接不定式 / 动名词含义不同

to do vs doing
try / remember / forget / regret / stop
try to do（尽力）/doing（尝试）
remember to do（记得要做）/doing（记得做过）
forget to do / doing · regret to do（遗憾地做）/ doing（后悔做过）
stop to do（停下做另一件）/ doing（停止正在做）
want / can't help / mean
want to do（想）/ doing（需要被做）
can't help doing（忍不住）/ help to do（帮忙）
mean to do（打算）/ doing（意味着）
cease / propose / quit / leave off
cease to do（永久）/ doing（暂时）
propose to do（打算）/ doing（建议）
quit to do（停下去做另一件）/ doing（停止正在做）
leave off to do / doing（同上）
deserve / learn / chance
deserve to do（值得/配）/ doing（该被）
learn to do（学会）/ doing（学习过程）
chance to do（碰巧）/ doing（冒险尝试）
经典对照
We want to paint the fence.（打算）
The fence wants painting.（需要）
The class meant to have a picnic.（打算）
Genius only means striving all one's life.（意味着）
The bus has ceased running.（暂时）
…has ceased to run.（永久）
更多对照
The criminal deserved shooting.（该被枪毙）
The student deserved to shoot first.（该第一个射击）
We have learned to speak skillfully.（学会了）
We have been learning skiing for some time.（学习）
Peter chanced to find the necklace.（碰巧）
Peter chanced swimming across the river.（冒险）

现在/将来设想用过去时（be 用 were）；过去设想用 had + 过去分词。
6. dream / believe / expect（should/could）
She never dreamed that she could see her mother at 60.
I don't expect that you should have told her the truth.
否定/疑问句中表惊讶、怀疑等，should/could 不可省。
7. would rather / would sooner / had better + 从句
I'd rather (that) I had a chance to visit you now.
We'd sooner she had passed the test last time.
I had better he were not.
现在/将来愿望用过去时；过去愿望用过去完成时。
8. Be it…or / though…be
Be it cheap or not, I will get it for my parents.
Be it true or not, I should keep some distance from him.
Though all the world be cold, I must be kind to all.
Though a merchant be nice, yet he never changes his intention of making money.
八 · 虚拟语气

考点22 · 虚拟语气用于其他场合

Subjunctive Uses

用于祝愿、诅咒、祈使，提出建议、劝告，提出请求、邀请。

God bless you!（bless 不加 s）
Heaven help us!（help 不加 s）
May all you be healthy and happy.
Liars be hanged!
So be it then!

九 · 倒装与反意

考点23 · 其他倒装结构

More Inversion
1. 副词/介词 + with + 名词（命令）
In with you, boys!
Up with the flowers, Tom!
Out with your ideas!
2. 行为动词 + 主语 + 情态/助动词（强调）
It's my duty to help you, and do it I will.
Go ahead I must.
Sing a song I cannot; let me give you a dance instead.
3. of 短语在句首
Of the thirty students, as many as ten passed.
Of how to protect animals…, the question remained unsettled.
4. ever / never 等前置（句末为 be/情态/助动词时）
We never have and never will retreat.
Adults all are. · The husband never is.
5. 比较/方式状语从句倒装
Mothers give more than will fathers.
He is honest, as are most high-achieving students.
Chinese students like to travel, as do foreign students.

九 · 倒装与反意

考点24 · 反意疑问句的几种特殊情况

Tag Questions
1. 陈述句含 only → 肯/否均可
There were only five people, weren't / were they?
Mr. Smith is the only person, isn't / is he?
2. 主语是 A+B → 视情况而定
You and I designed it, didn't we?（含 I 用 we）
Whisky and soda is popular, isn't it?（混合饮料看单数）
The love and care we feel…, isn't it?（同一概念看单数）
3. It is the first time that… → isn't it
It is the first time you've visited, isn't it?
It was the second time you had read the novel, wasn't it?
It is (high) time that we left, isn't it?

十一 · the way

考点35 · the way 的四重用法

The Way
1. 名词（= the manner 方式）
The way he speaks is very impressive.
I like the way she walks, lithe and graceful.
2. 副词（= how）
That is the way we did the job.
We want to know the way you learned the skill.
3. 连词（= as 像 / if 如果照这样下去）
Tom dealt with others the way his mother did.
The girl doesn't speak the way the boy does.
The way you are learning, you won't read like a native.
4. in the way + 句子（以某种方式）
They cooked chicken in the way that reminded me of my grandma.
「in the way + 句子」中 in 不可省略。

收尾

本章小结

Summary

否定（形式 ↔ 意义）

  • not…because：否定转移 / 不转移。
  • not A and B：否定 B；not some：部分否定。
  • cannot…too / 否定词+比较级 / no…but：意义肯定。
  • more than…can / anything but：意义否定。

强调（十种手段）

  • 重复名词/动词/数词、the last、shall。
  • at all / on earth / nothing but / anything but。
  • 形容词后置 / as…as / do+原形 / none of。

非谓语 & 虚拟

  • come to do（逐渐）、to have done（虚拟主语）。
  • remember/forget/stop/mean + to do / doing。
  • 名词化动名词（有复数）。
  • It is essential that…(should)、lest / for fear that。

从句 & 其他

  • 定语从句状语化（因果/结果/让步）。
  • there be 定语从句、that 让步倒装。
  • 条件的其他表达（祈使句+and/or、but for）。
  • the way（名词/副词/连词）。
