数词 · 总览

数词：表“多少”与“第几”

Overview
one

基数词 Cardinal

表示数量多少（多少个）
one, two, three …
twenty-one, hundred, thousand, million, billion
1st

序数词 Ordinal

表示顺序先后（第几个）
first, second, third …
the first（第一）· a fourth（又一个）
🔑
数词在句中可作：主语（Thirty of them…）· 宾语（pass me the second）· 表语（He was the fourth to arrive）· 定语（the ninth boy）· 同位语（We two will go with you，仅限基数词）。
💡
记忆抓手：记数词先记「两大类」→ 再记「三张表」（基数词表、序数词表、应用表）→ 最后用「分数 / 小数 / 百分数」三块收尾。
考点 1 · 基数词

基数词的构成规则

Cardinal Numbers
数字范围构成规则例词
0 ~ 12各自独立，无规律zero 0 · one 1 · nine 9 · twelve 12
13 ~ 193~9 加 -teen 结尾特殊：thirteen 13 · fifteen 15 · eighteen 18
一般：fourteen 14 · sixteen 16
20 ~ 90 整十以 -ty 结尾twenty 20 · thirty 30 · forty 40 · fifty 50 · sixty 60 · seventy 70 · eighty 80 · ninety 90
两位数（几十几）整十位数 + 连字符 + 个位数twenty-one 21 · thirty-five 35 · ninety-eight 98
三位数（几百几）…hundred and + 两位数seven hundred and eighty-five 785 · two hundred and ten 210
多位数从右向左每三位加逗号，依次为 thousand / million / billion76,832,154,968 → seventy-six billion, eight hundred and thirty-two million, one hundred and fifty-four thousand, nine hundred and sixty-eight
⚠️
易错：具体数字后的 hundred / thousand / million / billion 不加 s（eight hundred，不是 hundreds）；只有表“成百上千”的泛指才用 hundreds of / thousands of / millions of。
考点 2 · 序数词

序数词的变化规则

Ordinal Numbers
数字范围变化规则例词
1, 2, 3不规则变化first 第一 · second 第二 · third 第三
4 ~ 19基数词词尾加 -th一般：fourth 第四 · nineteenth 第十九
特殊：fifth 第五 · eighth 第八 · ninth 第九 · twelfth 第十二
20 ~ 90 整十把 -ty 变 -tie，再加 -thtwenty → twentieth 第二十 · ninety → ninetieth 第九十
两位数 / 多位数（除整数外）只变个位数为序数词，hundred / thousand 等只在词尾加 -thtwenty-third 第二十三 · one hundred and fifty-ninth 第一百五十九 · hundredth 第一百
💡
序数词简写：阿拉伯数字 + 序数词最后两个字母 → 1st、2nd、3rd、4th、5th（first、second、third 特殊，其余多 -th）。
考点 3 · 序数词用法

基本用法 · 省略 the

Usage of Ordinals ①
the

加 the 表顺序

the first book 第一本书
He lives on the second floor. 他住在二楼。
I come first. 我得了第一名。
a / an

加 a/an 表“又一、再一”

They still want a fourth one.
尽管已有三个孩子，还想再要一个。
a second = 又一次 / 再来一次
省略 the 的三种情况
① 表日期June 1st（1st 前的 the 省略）
② 固定搭配at first（起初）· be second to · Second Avenue
③ 物主代词 / 所有格Tom's third time（前面已有 Tom's，不加 the）
🔑
一句话：序数词前该加 the，但遇到物主代词 / 名词所有格、日期、固定搭配就省掉 the。例：his fortieth birthday（他的四十岁生日，his 后面不出现 the）。
考点 3 · 序数词用法

复数形式 · 搭配 · 位置

Usage of Ordinals ②
1

复数形式

表示“第几组 / 第几类”概念
the fifths = 五分之一的人或事物（将整体分五等份中的一份）
2

与其他词搭配

可搭配形容词 / 副词 / 介词短语
second-best 第二好的 · third-largest 第三大的 · in the first place 首先 · at the second attempt 第二次尝试时
3

在句中的位置

通常位于名词 / 代词之前
序数词 + 名词构成名词短语
the first book · the ninth boy
🎯
高频考点聚焦：“a/an + 序数词 = 再一、又一”（a second / a fourth）是改错和语法填空的常客，务必和“the + 序数词 = 第几个”区别开。
考点 4 · 数词应用

日期 · 年份 · 年代

Dates & Years
名称表达法例词
年份日期
（月·日·年）		 日用序数词，年用基数词；年份可先读前两位再读后两位，读“日”时要加 the March the twenty-first, nineteen ninety-seven
1997年3月21日
年份日期
（日·月·年）		 the + 序数词 + of + 月，年 the twenty-first of March, two thousand and seven
2007年3月21日
年代 in + the + 数词复数形式（加 -s 或 's） in the 1720's / 1720s
读作 in the seventeen twenties，18世纪20年代
💡
年代高频：“二十世纪九十年代” = the 1990s / the nineties；“在他八十多岁时” = in his eighties（注意介词 in + 物主代词 his）。
考点 4 · 数词应用

时刻 · 编号

Time & Numbering
名称表达法例词
顺读时在前，分在后，按基数词读10:15 → ten fifteen
past（过）分在前时在后，“几点过几分”用 past，分钟数 ≤ 半小时7:05 five past seven · 7:15 a quarter past seven · 7:30 half past seven
to（差）“几点差几分”用 to，分钟数在半小时以内（不含半小时）7:57 three to eight · 11:46 fourteen to twelve · 2:40 twenty to three
刻钟一刻钟 a quarter，三刻钟 three quarters九点一刻 → a quarter past nine
编号序号在名词前用序数词，在名词后用基数词第12课 → the twelfth lesson = Lesson Twelve
⚠️
编号陷阱：“Gate Four / Lesson Twelve”是名词 + 基数词（不加 the、不用序数词）；而“the twelfth lesson”才是the + 序数词 + 名词。两者都对，别混着用。
考点 4 · 数词应用

分数 · 倍数

Fractions & Multiples
名称表达法例词
分数分子用基数词，分母用序数词；分子 > 1 时分母加 -s1/3 one third · 2/3 two thirds · 4/5 four fifths · 7/10 seven tenths
特殊分数1/2 用 a/one half，1/4 用 a/one quartera half · a quarter
带分数整数 + and + 分数2¾ → two and three fourths
倍数两倍用 twice，两倍以上用基数词 + timestwice 两倍 · three times 三倍 · forty times 四十倍
🔑
记忆口诀：「分子基，分母序；分子大于 1，分母加 s」—— 例：三分之二 = two thirds（two 是基数，third 变复数 thirds）。
考点 5 · 小数与百分数

小数 · 百分数

Decimals & Percent
point

小数

小数点读 point，点前按基数词、点后逐位读数字
0.5 → zero point five / point five
12.12 → twelve point one two
12.297 → twelve point two nine seven
The price is 19.99 dollars.（表价格、尺寸）
%

百分数

基数词 + percent，直接读 percent
25% → twenty-five percent

作主语：Ninety percent of the students passed.
作宾语：invested twenty percent
作定语：a thirty percent increase
作表语：The pass rate is sixty percent.
💡
区别记忆：小数读 point（逐位读），百分数读 percent（整体读）；两者都可作主语、宾语、定语、表语。
实战 · 专项练习

单项选择 ①（1–6）

Multiple Choice
1. There are ___ days in a week and Tuesday is ___ day of the week.
A. seven, third　B. seven, the third　C. seventh, three　D. the seventh, three
▸ 答案
B
“七天”用基数 seven；“第二天”序数词前加 the third。
2. — There are 60 students in my class and 44 of them use Weibo very often.
— 44? That means about ___ of your classmates are Weibo users, right?
A. half　B. one third　C. two thirds　D. three quarters
▸ 答案
D
44/60 ≈ 75% → three quarters（四分之三）。
3. Eight ___ students in our school are from the countryside.
A. hundreds　B. hundred　C. hundreds of　D. hundred of
▸ 答案
B
具体数字 eight 后的 hundred 不加 s、不加 of。
4. He wrote his ___ novel when he was ___.
A. five, fifties　B. fifth, fifty　C. fifth, fiftieth　D. five, fiftieth
▸ 答案
B
“第五本小说”用序数 fifth；“他五十岁”用基数 fifty。
5. July 1st, 2011 is ___ birthday of the Communist Party of China.
A. ninety　B. the ninety　C. the ninetieth　D. ninetieth
▸ 答案
C
“第九十个生日”= the ninetieth（序数词 + the）。
6. Kangkang is a school boy. He got a good gift on his ___ birthday.
A. fourteenth　B. fortieth　C. the fortieth　D. the fourteenth
▸ 答案
A
物主代词 his 后省略 the；学生约 14 岁 → fourteenth。
实战 · 专项练习

单项选择 ②（7–12）

Multiple Choice
7. I don't believe that this ___ boy can paint such a nice picture.
A. five years old　B. five-years-old　C. five-year-old　D. fifth-year-old
▸ 答案
C
复合形容词作定语用连字符、名词用单数：five-year-old。
8. About ___ of the students in Grade Nine this year were born in the ___.
A. three five, 1996　B. three fifths, 1990s　C. third fifth, 1997　D. third fifths, 1990s
▸ 答案
B
“五分之三”分子 > 1 分母加 s → three fifths；年代 the 1990s。
9. — If a=4, b=5, what's the answer to the question “a²+2ab+1”?
A. Forty-fifth　B. Forty-five　C. Twenty-three　D. One hundred and twenty-one
▸ 答案
B
数学题答案读作基数词 forty-five（45），不用序数词 Forty-fifth。
10. — Do you have enough students to clean the laboratory?
— No, I think we need ___ students.
A. another　B. two others　C. more two　D. two more
▸ 答案
D
“还需要两个”= 数词 + more：two more students。
11. ___ reward was offered for the capture of the escaped criminals.
A. A five-thousand-dollars　B. A five-thousand-dollar　C. The five-thousands-dollar　D. Five thousand-dollar
▸ 答案
B
复合形容词 five-thousand-dollar（单数），前面加 a。
12. The salesgirl remembered clearly the fashionable woman ordered ___ value of software.
A. fifty dollars　B. fiftieth dollars'　C. fifty dollar　D. fifty dollars'
▸ 答案
D
“五十美元的价值”= 复数所有格 fifty dollars' value。

单项选择 ③（13–18）

Multiple Choice
13. That was ___ desk, for there was no room for ___ in their study.
A. Susan and her sister's, a second　B. Susan's and her sister's, the second　C. Susan and her sister's, the second　D. Susan's and her sister's, a second
▸ 答案
A
两人共有的桌子只在最后加 's：Susan and her sister's；“再放一张”= a second。
14. — I've been laid off, and my boss gave me only ___ notice.
— I'm sorry to hear that.
A. six-day　B. a week's　C. a weeks'　D. five day's
▸ 答案
B
“一周的通知”= a week's notice（名词所有格）。
15. She asked for leave to visit her mother and was granted ___.
A. a forty-eight-hours leave　B. the leave of forty-eight hour's　C. a forty-eight hours' leave　D. the forty-eight hour's leave
▸ 答案
C
“48 小时的假”= 复数所有格 forty-eight hours' leave。
16. The railway station is quite near: only ___ from the hotel.
A. ten-minutes drive　B. ten minute's drive　C. ten minutes' drive　D. ten-minute-drive
▸ 答案
C
“十分钟车程”= ten minutes' drive（复数所有格）。
17. — Is your home far away from the subway station?
— Well, not so far. It's ___ walk.
A. ten minutes　B. ten-minutes　C. ten minutes'　D. ten-minutes'
▸ 答案
C
“十分钟路程”= ten minutes' walk。
18. With an initial investment of only ten thousand dollars, he has increased his money ___.
A. by two thousand more　B. by two thousand　C. at two thousand more　D. by two thousands
▸ 答案
B
“增加了两千”= by two thousand（by + 具体差额；thousand 不加 s）。

单项选择 ④（19–24）

Multiple Choice
19. We have run 3 shops, even though they are small, at 220 ___ Street. We are not able to open ___ one at present.
A. Second; a fourth　B. the second; fourth　C. second; the fourth　D. Second; a four
▸ 答案
A
街名“第二大街”大写 Second Street；“再开第四家”= a fourth。
20. Attention, please. Flight CA893 from Hong Kong is now arriving at ___.
A. Gate Four　B. The Gate Four　C. The Four Gate　D. Fourth Gate
▸ 答案
A
“四号门”编号 = 名词 + 基数词 Gate Four。
21. The terrible war broke out in ___ of ___ century.
A. the nineties, eighteenth　B. nineties, the eighteen　C. the nineties, the nineteenth　D. nineties, the ninteenth
▸ 答案
C
“十九世纪九十年代”= the nineties of the nineteenth century。
22. It's amazing that my grandfather started to learn how to use a computer ___, when he was already ___.
A. in the 1990, eighties　B. in the 1990's, in his eighties　C. in the 1990, in eighties　D. in 1990's, in the eighties
▸ 答案
B
“九十年代”= in the 1990s；“他八十多岁”= in his eighties。
23. Is it ___ supermarket on ___ Avenue, ___ Street?
A. the, the Fifth, the Ninth　B. a, Fifth, Ninth　C. the, Fifth, Ninth　D. a, Fifth, the Ninth
▸ 答案
B
泛指“一家超市”= a；街名 Fifth Avenue、Ninth Street 不加 the。
24. During ___, this famous scientist found the way of discovering the new chemical element.
A. the later 1920's　B. the late 1920s　C. later 1920's　D. late 1920s
▸ 答案
B
“20 世纪 20 年代末”= the late 1920s。

单项选择 ⑤（25–30）

Multiple Choice
25. The planet appears small because it is ___ miles away from us.
A. millions of　B. million of　C. millions　D. million
▸ 答案
A
表“数百万”的泛指用 millions of。

D

A. two and half an　B. two and a half　C. half and two　D. two a half

B
“两个半小时”= two and a half hours。
29. The Manchester City manager Mark Hughes has confirmed that Brazilian striker Robinho will be out of action for ___ weeks.
A. three other　B. another three　C. more three　D. other three
▸ 答案
B

30. Don't speak at once, boys and girls. ___ please.
A. Once at a time　B. One at a time　C. One at one time　D. Each at one time
▸ 答案

实战 · 复盘

数词六大易错点

Six Traps
1

hundred 不加 s

eight hundred（具体数字后）；hundreds of（泛指）
2

复合形容词用单数

five-year-old · five-thousand-dollar · five-dollar ties
3

复数所有格

ten minutes' walk · forty-eight hours' leave · fifty dollars' value
4

序数词前的 the

his fourteenth birthday（his 后省 the）· the first book
5

a/an + 序数词 = 再一

a second 又一次 · a fourth 第四个 · a fifth 五分之一
6

分数：分子基分母序

two thirds（分子 > 1 分母加 s）· one third
🎯
做题顺序：① 先判基数还是序数；② 再看修饰关系（复合形容词单数 / 复数所有格）；③ 最后查the、a/an、of 等固定搭配。

  • 分数：分子基分母序

小数百分数

  • 小数读 point
  • 百分数读 percent
  • 可作主 / 宾 / 定 / 表语
