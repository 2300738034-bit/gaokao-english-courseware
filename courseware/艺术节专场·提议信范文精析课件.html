PART 01 · 审题分析

写作六要素 · 先想清楚再动笔

Read the Task
📌
题目回顾：明启中学为丰富学校艺术节，拟在原有三个专场（书法 / 器乐 / 歌曲）之外再增加一个专场，现向师生征求意见。假设你是该校学生林平，给负责此事的王老师写一封电子邮件，提出你的意见。
维度审题要点
写作背景学校为丰富艺术节，新增一个专场，向师生征求意见
写作体裁电子邮件 · 提议信（proposal letter） ＝ 说明 + 议论
内容要求① 专场的名称 ② 专场的具体内容 ③ 增加该专场的理由
作者明启中学学生 · 林平
读者学校艺术节负责老师 · 王老师
写作目的说服王老师采纳意见、增加该专场
所需技能说明（把专场内容讲清楚）+ 议论（把增加理由讲充分）
💡
审题记住三点：写什么（名称＋内容＋理由，缺一不可）、写给谁（老师，语气要委婉）、为什么写（说服采纳）。翻到下一页看「说明 vs 议论」的两条关键提醒。
PART 01 · 审题分析

两条关键提醒 · 说明 vs 议论

Two Reminders
①说明部分 = 专场的具体内容；议论部分 = 增加专场的理由。说明要紧扣所提议专场的具体特点，避免泛泛而谈；议论部分尤其要讲清该专场在学生艺术节中的地位 / 作用，以及它与其他已有专场相比的独到之处。
②这是学生写给负责老师的提议信，语言要委婉而恳切，避免颐指气使的表达；可联系「艺术」话题的大背景来拔高立意，如人对艺术的感受、艺术对学校生活的作用等。
⚠️
易错点：不少同学把「说明内容」写得像说明书（只罗列流程），把「理由」写得太空（只说"很有意义"）。记住——说明要具体、理由要独到，这正是范文拿高分的地方。
审题口诀 —— 名称 · 内容 · 理由 三不缺，委婉 · 独到 两不忘
PART 02 · 范文精读

范文全文 · 先通读一遍

Read the Model
绿色高亮 = 优美 / 出彩表达 金色下划线 = 委婉表达 & 衔接副词 ①②③④⑤ = 逐段点评标记

Dear Mr/Mrs Wang,

①I'm excited to hear that our school is planning to add another section to the art festival apart from calligraphy, instruments and songs. I'm writing to voice my humble opinions, which will hopefully contribute to your decision.

②From my perspective, it is advisable to add a photography section, which features photos of various themes and styles shot by students. After a preliminary selection by photography teachers based on the professionality and meaningfulness of the works, a vote can be held on the school website for the students and teachers to select the best photos and prizes will be awarded to them. Those award-winning photos will then be exhibited in the school art gallery for everyone to appreciate.

③My recommendation for the photography section is based on the following reasons. Compared to calligraphy, instruments and songs, which greatly emphasize performers' specialized skills and momentary performances, photography more requires students' capability to observe and capture the tiny but touching moments in this fast-paced life. Therefore, photography is an art activity not limited to those with special talents, but open to all the students who like to reflect on reality.

④More importantly, its very presence at the school art festival encourages students to pay attention to the things and people around, and motivates them to find the hidden beauty in their seemingly repeated and tedious daily lives. Hopefully, students' aesthetic appreciation can be greatly improved and their spiritual world can be immensely enriched.

⑤In a nutshell, a photography section is a wise choice for the art festival, and I would appreciate it very much if my opinions could be taken into consideration. I am looking forward to your reply.

Yours,
Lin Ping

👀
先通读感受全文的「骨架」：开头表态 → 说明内容 → 理由 1 → 理由 2 → 总结请求。留意绿色与下划线的表达，翻到下一页逐段点评 ①—⑤。
PART 02 · 范文精读

点评 ①② · 开头表态 & 说明内容

Comment 1–2
① 开头开门见山 · 委婉表态
I'm excited to hear that our school is planning to add another section to the art festival apart from calligraphy, instruments and songs. I'm writing to voice my humble opinions, which will hopefully contribute to your decision.
点此查看点评＋
一句话同时点明写作背景（学校要新增专场）与写作目的（提出意见）；用 voice my humble opinions 表达委婉恳切，符合「学生写给老师的提议信」这一情境，避免了颐指气使。
② 说明被动语态 · 讲清专场内容
From my perspective, it is advisable to add a photography section, which features photos of various themes and styles shot by students. After a preliminary selection… a vote can be held… prizes will be awarded… will then be exhibited in the school art gallery.
点此查看点评＋
用被动语态（a vote can be held / prizes will be awarded / will then be exhibited）说明专场活动内容，流程清晰、重点突出；内容紧扣「摄影」——选片、投票、评奖、展出，环环相扣，具体不空洞。
✍️
「说明部分」的写法：把活动拆成几个步骤，用被动语态串成一条清晰的流程线，让读者一眼看懂「这个专场到底怎么办」。
PART 02 · 范文精读

点评 ③④ · 两个理由层层递进

Comment 3–4
③ 理由 1对比凸显独特性
Compared to calligraphy, instruments and songs, which greatly emphasize performers' specialized skills and momentary performances, photography more requires students' capability to observe and capture the tiny but touching moments in this fast-paced life. Therefore, photography is an art activity not limited to those with special talents, but open to all the students who like to reflect on reality.
点此查看点评＋
第一个理由：通过与其他三个专场对比，凸显摄影「观察并捕捉生活瞬间」的独到之处，说明在学校艺术节增加它的必要性。语法丰富：非谓语作状语（Compared to…）、限定性 / 非限定性定语从句（which…, who…）、非谓语作后置定语（shot by students）；tiny but touching 意义对比、语音押头韵，尤为出彩。
④ 理由 2拔高立意 · 意义升华
More importantly, its very presence… encourages students to pay attention to the things and people around, and motivates them to find the hidden beauty in their seemingly repeated and tedious daily lives. Hopefully, students' aesthetic appreciation can be greatly improved and their spiritual world can be immensely enriched.
点此查看点评＋
第二个理由：阐释该专场对学校艺术氛围的引导与鼓励作用，把立意从「办活动」拔高到「提升审美、丰富精神世界」。副词（More importantly / Hopefully / greatly / immensely）衔接自然、表意丰富，值得借鉴。
🎯
两个理由有层次：理由 1 讲「它跟别的专场有什么不同」（独特性），理由 2 讲「它对学生有什么好处」（意义）。一个具体、一个升华，缺一不可。
PART 02 · 范文精读

点评 ⑤ · 总结 & 综合评价

Comment 5 + Review
⑤ 结尾高度总结 · 委婉请求
In a nutshell, a photography section is a wise choice for the art festival, and I would appreciate it very much if my opinions could be taken into consideration. I am looking forward to your reply.
点此查看点评＋
总结前文：因为前面说明具体、理由充分，句中 a wise choice 才言之有物、掷地有声；结尾用 if my opinions could be taken into consideration 委婉提出请求，得体收束。
综合评价整篇好在哪里
点此查看综合评价＋
本文结构清晰、层次分明、篇幅合理：说明部分具体明确，议论部分充分有力；字里行间流露出作者对摄影的热爱与了解，以及对增加该专场的殷切盼望。结尾在说明与议论的基础上高度总结，与主题形成照应。除大量优美词汇外，作者还运用丰富多样的句式与语法结构，使内容重点突出、衔接流畅。
结构骨架 —— 开头表态 → 说明内容 → 理由 1 → 理由 2 → 总结请求
PART 03 · 好词好句

三句背诵级表达 · 点击揭晓讲解

Good Sentences
⭐
这三句是范文里最值得背诵的句子——一句引出建议，一句写「独特」，一句写「对比」。先读英文，自己想想好在哪，再点开讲解对照。
1. From my perspective, it is advisable to add a photography section, which features photos of various themes and styles shot by students.
点此查看讲解＋
from my perspective ＝ 在我看来（替代 in my opinion，更书面）；it is advisable to do sth. ＝ 做某事是明智的（提建议常用句式）；feature（动词）＝ 以……为特点；photos of various themes and styles ＝ 各种主题与风格的照片。
2. Photography more requires students' capability to observe and capture the tiny but touching moments in this fast-paced life.
点此查看讲解＋
tiny 与 touching 共同修饰 moments：意义上形成对比、语音上押头韵（alliteration），增强语言的美感与冲击力；observe and capture 观察并捕捉；fast-paced life 快节奏的生活。
3. Therefore, photography is an art activity not limited to those with special talents, but open to all the students who like to reflect on reality.
点此查看讲解＋
not… but… ＝ 不是……而是……，通过对比凸显摄影的独特之处，有力阐释增加专场的必要性；reflect on reality ＝ 反思现实。

PART 04 · 表达积累

翻译填空 · 把括号中文译成英语

Translate
🧩
先把整句读顺，再想括号里的中文用英语怎么表达。点开对照参考译文——翻译没有唯一答案，言之成理即可。
1
It will 〔使学生充分意识到〕 that art is capable of 〔通过视觉和听觉感动观众〕, and 〔唤起情绪反应〕 in them.
点此查看参考译文＋
It will make students fully aware that art is capable of moving the audience through sight and hearing, and arousing emotional responses in them.
注意 be capable of + doing；「使学生充分意识到」用 make… fully aware。
2
Calling for great patience and a peaceful mind, painting may get students to 〔培养一个爱好〕 enabling them to be 〔精神放松〕 and 〔暂时远离压力巨大的学校课业〕.
点此查看参考译文＋
…painting may get students to develop a hobby enabling them to be mentally relaxed and temporarily away from the heavy pressure of schoolwork.
「培养爱好」develop / cultivate a hobby；「精神放松」mentally relaxed；「暂时远离」temporarily away from。
3
Drawing activities 〔在现场〕 may also 〔激发我们的兴趣〕 and broaden our experience by 〔提供动手的机会〕.
点此查看参考译文＋
Drawing activities on the spot may also arouse our interest and broaden our experience by providing hands-on opportunities.
「在现场」on the spot；「激发兴趣」arouse / stimulate our interest；「提供动手机会」providing hands-on opportunities。
PART 04 · 表达积累

表达训练 · 用关键词翻译整句

Use the Word
🏋️
仿照范文的句式，用括号里的关键词把中文句子译成英语。点开对照参考译文，注意关键词的用法。
1
除了审美乐趣，画画还可以丰富我们的精神世界。pleasure
点此查看参考译文＋
Besides the aesthetic pleasure, painting can also enrich our spiritual world.
「审美乐趣」aesthetic pleasure；「丰富精神世界」enrich our spiritual world。
2
在欣赏芭蕾舞的时候，观众们可能也会觉得自己好像坐在一条宁静的河边。feel like
点此查看参考译文＋
While enjoying the ballet, the audience may also feel like sitting beside a quiet river.
feel like + doing；「宁静的河边」beside a quiet river。
3
综上所述，绘画专场将会为我们即将到来的艺术节添上浓墨重彩的一笔。touch
点此查看参考译文＋
In conclusion, the painting section will add a brilliant touch to our upcoming art festival.
「添上浓墨重彩的一笔」add a brilliant touch to…；「综上所述」In conclusion / To sum up。
本课小结

结构模板 & 背诵清单

Takeaway
🏗 提议信 · 五步结构模板
1开头：点明背景 + 委婉表态（I'm excited to hear… / voice my humble opinions）
2提议：名称 + 特点（From my perspective, it is advisable to add…）
3说明内容：被动语态串起清晰流程（a vote can be held / will be awarded / exhibited）
4理由：两个层次——独特性（Compared to…）+ 意义拔高（More importantly…）
5结尾：总结 + 委婉请求（In a nutshell… / I would appreciate it if…）
📝 背诵清单 · 必背表达
短语
From my perspective / it is advisable to do sth.
动词
feature（v.）· photos of various themes and styles
押韵
observe and capture the tiny but touching moments
对比
not limited to…, but open to…
衔接
More importantly / In a nutshell / a wise choice
收尾
I would appreciate it very much if…
