PART 01 · 概述

什么是概要写作

What is Summary Writing
读

读懂文章

300 词左右原文，掌握内容结构、段间句间逻辑，领会主旨与意图
写

写出概要

60 词以内，用简明语言确切表述原文重要内容
禁

两大禁区

不加主观评论和解释；不照抄原文原句
💡
概要写作 = 读（筛选关键信息） + 写（高度概括表达），最终文本要既「概」也「要」——既概括，又抓住要点。
📌
上海卷 2017 年起一年两考新增题型，卷面分值 10 分，同时考查阅读能力与语言表达能力，是读写结合的「综合题」。
PART 01 · 概述

写概要的两大步

Two Steps
①

读：阅读与圈划

1第一遍略读（半分钟）：圈划结构信号词与逻辑信号词
2第二遍细读（两分钟）：区分主次信息，圈划论据与叙事关键节点
3不同词句用不同记号，便于下一步动笔
②

写：整合与表达

4用句式转换 / 同义替换 / 词性转换 / 句子整合 / 长句缩短整合信息
5用连接词让结构更紧凑
6认真检查：语言准确、语义连贯
先「读」圈重点，再「写」精压缩 —— 两遍读 + 五招写
PART 01 · 概述

六大要点（上）

Key Points 1-3
要点 1 · 时间与习惯
略读半分钟内、细读两分钟内完成；平时养成边读边圈划的习惯，培养对逻辑信号词的敏感度，并能迅速判断代词的指代对象。
要点 2 · 主次信息
按原文顺序总结；主要信息常有拓展句 / 例子深入分析，次要信息篇幅少。注意：not… instead of… 结构中信息一般非重点，而 Instead / However 之后要重视；例子要按篇幅酌情判断是否保留。
要点 3 · 用自己的话
不引用原句；核心名词保留，阐释性、描述性文字用同义替换 / 句型转换加工处理。
PART 01 · 概述

六大要点（下）

Key Points 4-6
要点 4 · 最短连接词
尽量用 but / then / thus / yet / though；少用 at the same time / in the first place / because of these / on the other hand 等长连接词；可用分号代替连接词。
要点 5 · 重视标题
文章标题通常提纲挈领地表达中心意思，必须重视其提供的信息。
要点 6 · 并列结构
并列结构引出的信息同等重要，不可偏颇一方。
💡
记忆口诀：限时读 · 分主次 · 自写话 · 短连接 · 重标题 · 并列全
PART 01 · 概述

评分标准

Scoring Criteria

内容（五档 A–E）

A准确全面概括主旨，涵盖主要信息
B准确概括，但遗漏个别主要信息
C概括主旨，但遗漏部分主要信息
D未能准确概括，遗漏较多或留过多细节
E几乎不能概括主旨，未涉及有意义信息

语言（五档 A–E）

A用自己的语言连贯、正确表述
B较连贯正确，有个别语言错误
C连贯性较差，有少量不影响表意的错误
D连贯性差，严重语言错误较多
E几乎不能连贯正确表述
🎯
得分 = 内容全面（不漏主要信息） × 语言地道（自己的话、连贯正确）。两者都要冲 A 档。
PART 02 · 分类总览

两类文章

Two Text Types
A

叙述类文章

Narrative
讲述一个故事，按情节发展推进，涵盖 5W1H（Who / What / When / Where / Why / How）。
B

论说类文章

Argumentative
围绕观点 / 现象 / 问题 / 研究 / 建议展开，分六种亚型，各有固定结构套路。
💡
拿到文章先「定性」：是叙事还是论说？再套用对应的结构模板去圈划，事半功倍。
PART 03 · 叙述类文章

情节发展的五个阶段

Five-stage Plot
①

引入

Exposition · 故事开端，交代背景（时间、地点、人物、矛盾）
②

发展

Rising action · 矛盾冲突不断变化发展的过程
③

高潮

Climax · 冲突的转折点，故事最紧张的部分
④

结局

Falling action · 故事发展结局，矛盾的最终解决
⑤

尾声

Resolution · 结局的影响与作者的总结、思考
自检

5W1H

Who · What · When · Where · Why · How
🔑
「尾声」并非情节发展的必要部分，并非所有叙述类文章都具备。前四阶段是主干。
PART 03 · 叙述类文章

叙述类 · 写作四步

Four Steps
1按情节发展阶段，把文本分成 四到五个部分
2圈划符合该部分功能、推动情节发展的主要内容，每部分用一句话总结
3用精炼的语言连接、润色每个部分的概述句
4认真检查，自检标准：5W1H（Who / What / When / Where / Why / How）
💡
核心是「一句话概括一个阶段」，最后把五句话用连接词串起来就是概要。
PART 03 · 叙述类 · 例题

例题精读（上）· 圈划关键信息

Meet the Member Who Quit Sugar

① 引入 · Exposition
When I was 15, I weighed 100 kg. I was a size 20+ and hated the way I looked. I was a serial big eater and quite depressed. I would sit in front of the fridge, eat something, move to the cupboard, eat a packet of chips and move back to the fridge. It was a heartbreaking cycle of hate and food. I didn't feel confident, pretty, or worthwhile. Shopping made me cry, I avoided mirrors, and my social life came to an end. I actually lost friends because they told me I was antisocial.

▸ 为何提炼：交代背景与困境（who/what + 矛盾）——开篇即点明「谁、什么处境」；坐冰箱前吃、躲镜子、失友等是具体描写（次要信息），可压缩省略。

② 发展 · Rising action
But then, I turned my life around. I quit sugar. It started with my mum. She staged an intervention that made me cry. She put me onto a diet that mostly aimed to schedule meals, and — to my shock horror — cut out all processed sugar. It was so hard at first. I remember the shame of sneaking chocolate and candies when the three o'clock hunger hits.

▸ 为何提炼：推动情节的关键转折——「妈妈介入、要求戒糖」改变故事走向；偷吃巧克力、嘴馋等是次要细节，不写入。
💡
高亮为圈划的「主要信息」，其余为次要细节（可压缩或省略）。阅读时先给文章分段，再逐段抓关键。
PART 03 · 叙述类 · 例题

例题精读（下）· 圈划关键信息

Meet the Member Who Quit Sugar

③ 高潮 · Climax
But the weeks kept coming and it just got easier. I stopped eating sugar, and I stopped loving it; simple as that. I substituted chips and biscuits for fruit and nuts, and had cup-of-soups after school. I also had more energy, having a 20-minute walk with my dog into my everyday routine; I began to love that personal time.

▸ 为何提炼：矛盾转化的结果——「逐渐适应健康饮食」是转折后的核心状态；吃水果坚果、遛狗等具体做法（次要），用「健康饮食」一词概括即可。

④ 结局 · Falling action
Now I'm 17, turning 18, and I weigh 70 kg. I still struggle with my weight, but this is where I sit naturally, and I am very proud of myself. I never thought I would be confident enough to wear dresses or skirts, and take leadership roles to put myself out there and develop my social skills.

▸ 为何提炼：结局（结果）——「体重下降、重获自信」是冲突的最终解决；穿裙子、担任领导等是例证（次要），可省略。

⑤ 尾声 · Resolution
I could never have done it without the support of my friends and family, and I urge everyone who wants to change their diet — quitting sugar or otherwise — to share their decision with people. Develop a support network, online or offline, which can offer you advice or pick you up when you're down. Your body will thank you, and you will thank yourself too.

▸ 为何提炼：作者的建议/总结——「采取行动、寻求支持」是尾声的主旨句；感谢亲友等是次要展开，不写入。
📏
原文 446 词，最终概要仅 59 词。压缩的关键：每阶段只留一句话，删掉细节描写与例子。
PART 03 · 叙述类 · 例题

概括句对照 · 点击揭示

Summary Sentence by Sentence
概括句（点击逐条显示 + 转写逻辑）
① 引入 · 概括句＋
Before quitting sugar, she was low-spirited about her overweight.
▸ 转写：信息概括（weighed 100 kg / size 20+ / depressed → low-spirited about her overweight）+ 句式转换（Before quitting sugar 时间状语前置）
② 发展 · 概括句＋
Then her mom stepped in and required her to follow a diet to quit sugar.
▸ 转写：信息概括（staged an intervention + put me onto a diet → stepped in, required a diet）+ 连接词 then 表时间顺序
③ 高潮 · 概括句＋
Despite initial discomfort, she was gradually used to eating and living healthily.
▸ 转写：句式转换（It was so hard at first → Despite initial discomfort 介词短语）+ 信息概括（substituted chips… → eating and living healthily）
④ 结局 · 概括句＋
With her weight lost, she has felt better and regained confidence.
▸ 转写：信息概括（70 kg / proud / confident → weight lost, felt better, regained confidence）+ 句式转换（with 介词短语）
⑤ 尾声 · 概括句＋
To do this right, we need to take action and seek support.
▸ 转写：信息概括（share the decision + support network → take action and seek support）+ 句式转换（不定式 to do this right 表目的）
合成范文（点击显示）
Possible version · 59 words＋
Before quitting sugar, she was low-spirited about her overweight. Then her mom stepped in and required her to follow a diet to quit sugar. Despite initial discomfort, she was gradually used to eating and living healthily. With her weight lost, she has felt better and regained confidence. To do this right, we need to take action and seek support.
💡
五句话 = 五个阶段，用 then / despite / with / to do 等连接词串起，顺序与原文一致。

论说类 1/6 · 现象分析型

现象分析型 · 结构与步骤

Phenomenon Analysis
文本结构内容要点说明
第一部分 · 呈现现象现象的具体表现点明「什么现象」
第二部分 · 分析说明主题句 + 支持性细节（方式、影响等）具体例子和数据不写入；总结性的展开说明要写
第三部分 · 评论积极 / 负面 / 谨慎的评论如与前文重复可不写；可能不出现
写作步骤
1按基本结构把文本分为三个部分
2圈划各部分内容要点
3撰写概要：现象关键信息 + 分析主题句与细节 + 评论
4精炼连接润色，检查：现象表现 + 主题句 + 支持细节关键词
论说类 1/6 · 例题

Gene Therapy

现象分析型
原文 · 圈划关键信息（含为何提炼）
① 现象
Scientists are seeing that gene therapy is revolutionizing the treatment of disease.▸ 为何：全文核心主旨句，点明「基因治疗革新疾病治疗」这一现象——概括的第一句必选。
② 现象
In gene therapy, healthy genes are introduced into defective cells to prevent or cure disease.▸ 为何：对现象的定义性说明（healthy genes→defective cells），核心名词须保留。
③ 主题句
While much of the research is in the beginning stages, some successes point to the real benefit of the therapy.▸ 为何：第二部分的主题句，概括「研究尚在初期、但已见成效」——统领后文细节。
④ 细节1
In Italy, doctors have treated one genetic disease... children now lead a relatively normal life.▸ 为何：支持主题句的实例结论（儿童→正常生活）；具体治疗过程为次要，省略。
⑤ 细节2
Those treated showed a 23.1 percent improvement when tested six months later. （数据·次要，不写）▸ 为何：具体数据，属次要信息，不写入概要。
⑥ 评论
Researchers hope every genetic disease will have gene therapy as its treatment. But more research is needed to assure its safety.▸ 为何：第三部分的评论/结论（谨慎态度），作为概要的收尾句。
概括 · 点击揭示（含转写逻辑）
第一部分 · 现象＋
Gene therapy is changing the treatment of disease revolutionarily.
▸ 转写：词性转换（revolutionizing v → revolutionarily adv）+ 句子整合（非谓语短语作定语，合并①②两句）
第二部分 · 分析＋
By inserting healthy genes into faulty cells, it benefits both children and older patients.
▸ 转写：同义替换（be introduced into → insert into；defective → faulty；beginning stages → initial phases）+ 信息概括（意大利儿童 + 老年患者 → both children and older patients）
第三部分 · 评论＋
However, more research should be done to ensure it's safe.
▸ 转写：句式转换（被动 needed → 主动 should be done）+ 同义替换（assure → ensure；safety → safe）+ 连接词 However 表转折
合成范文 · 54 words＋
Gene therapy, intended to prevent or cure disease by inserting healthy genes into faulty cells, is changing the treatment of disease revolutionarily. Though still in the initial phases, gene therapy has benefited both children and older patients with genetic disease by improving their conditions. However, more research should be done to ensure it's safe.
论说类 2/6 · 因果关系型

因果关系型 · 结构与步骤

Cause & Effect
文本结构内容要点说明
第一部分 · 呈现现象现象的具体表现 + 引出「原因」「Why」是核心
第二部分 · 原因每个原因的主题句 + 支持性细节一般两到三个原因，并具体展开
第三部分 · 总结对全文内容的总结可能不出现在文本中
写作步骤
1按基本结构把文本分为两到三个部分
2圈划各部分内容要点
3撰写概要：现象 + 引出原因 → 每个原因主题句与细节 → 总结
4连接润色，检查：现象主题句 + 每个原因的主题句与展开
论说类 2/6 · 例题 1

Shyness

因果关系型
原文 · 圈划关键信息（含为何提炼）
① 现象
Shyness is a universal phenomenon. Social scientists are exploring its environmental causes.▸ 为何：主旨句 + 引出原因，统领全文三个环境原因——是概括首句，三个原因都要点到。
② 原因1
The first environmental cause of shyness may be a child's home and family life. Children grow up in smaller families, with working parents, lacking socializing experience.▸ 为何：原因1 的主题句；smaller families / working parents 是解释「如何产生」的关键词，须保留。
③ 原因2
A second environmental cause of shyness may be one's culture. In Japan, failure is entirely blamed on the person, so Japanese learn not to take risks in public.▸ 为何：原因2 的主题句；「失败归咎个人」是解释文化的关键；57% 等数据次要，不写。
④ 原因3
Technology may also play a role. Watching TV, playing video games and surfing the Web have replaced social interaction.▸ 为何：原因3 的主题句；「科技替代社交」是核心；电视/游戏/银行机器等例证次要。
概括 · 点击揭示（含转写逻辑）
现象 + 引出原因＋
Shyness is common and it has environmental causes, including home and family life, culture and technology.
▸ 转写：同义替换（universal → common）+ 信息概括（三个原因压缩成 including… 并列列举）
原因 1 · 家庭＋
Firstly, with smaller families and working parents, children lack social interactions.
▸ 转写：句式转换（grow up in smaller families → with 介词短语）+ 词性转换（lack n → lack v）+ 同义替换（socializing experience → social interactions）
原因 2 · 文化＋
Secondly, blaming failure on individuals causes people in some cultures to be shy in public.
▸ 转写：句式转换（被动 blamed on the person → 主动 blaming failure on individuals）+ 同义替换（person → individual）+ 信息概括（Japanese → people in some cultures）
原因 3 · 科技＋
Finally, with the development of technology, people have fewer opportunities to socialize in person.
▸ 转写：信息概括（TV / video games / Web → the development of technology）+ 同义替换（replaced social interaction → fewer opportunities to socialize in person）
合成范文 · 56 words＋
Shyness is common and it has environmental causes, including home and family life, culture and technology. Firstly, with smaller families and working parents, children lack social interactions. Secondly, blaming failure on individuals causes people in some cultures to be shy in public. Finally, with the development of technology, people have fewer opportunities to socialize in person.
论说类 2/6 · 例题 2

Learning by Rote in the Digital Age

因果关系型
原文 · 圈划关键信息（含为何提炼）
① 现象
The decline of rote learning has been quickened by technology. But now there are voices for a need to return to rote learning.▸ 为何：现象 + 引出原因，点明「死记硬背被技术削弱、但价值被重新发现」——含转折，是概括首句的核心。
② 原因1
Memorizing key data is essential to learning any skill. Without memorizing these foundation elements, you cannot progress to a deeper understanding.▸ 为何：原因1 的主题句，说明死记硬背的价值（打基础）；医生/律师是例证，次要。
③ 原因2
Mobile learning can help bring this style of learning back to life by making it more convenient and fun, and keep us motivated.▸ 为何：原因2 的主题句，技术反哺死记硬背（更便捷有趣）；flashcards/drills 是具体手段，次要。
④ 总结
Once you've acquired the essentials by rote learning, you will find it easier to go deeper in the application of knowledge.▸ 为何：总结句，呼应原因1（掌握基础→深入应用）；内容与原因1重复时可不单独写。
概括 · 点击揭示（含转写逻辑）
现象 + 引出原因＋
Rote learning means mechanical memorization. Although technology makes it less needed, its value is being rediscovered.
▸ 转写：句子整合（三个简单句 → 让步状语从句 although）+ 句式转换（被动 quickened by technology → 主动 technology makes）+ 同义替换（decline → less needed；voices for a need to return → rediscover；essential → value）
原因 1 · 基础＋
Rote learning helps people acquire basic knowledge, laying a foundation for further understanding.
▸ 转写：句式转换（现在分词 laying a foundation 作伴随状语）+ 同义替换（memorize foundation elements → acquire basic knowledge；deeper → further）
原因 2 · 移动学习＋
Technology makes mobile learning possible, which motivates learners and makes memorization more interesting and individualized.
▸ 转写：句式转换（which 定语从句连接）+ 同义替换（convenient and fun → interesting；individual study → individualized）
合成范文 · 49 words＋
Rote learning means mechanical memorization. Although technology makes it less needed, its value is being rediscovered. Intervene Properly

问题解决型
原文 · 圈划关键信息（含为何提炼）
① 问题
A tired parent loses it — and a child suffers. We'd like to help, but we hesitate — will we embarrass and offend the parent?▸ 为何：问题的具体内容（孩子受虐、旁观者犹豫）——概括首句，交代「什么问题」。
② 反应
There is a common assumption that intervening is rude and critical. But it needn't be.▸ 为何：人们犹豫的原因/顾虑（误以为干涉=粗鲁批评），是承上启下的关键。
③ 方案
Intervention can be helpful and caring, not hurtful criticism. Children are too helpless and inexperienced to stand up for themselves, so we have an obligation to step in.▸ 为何：解决方案主题句（善意干涉）+ 理由（孩子无力自保）；具体问句「How dare you…」是例证，次要。
④ 展开
Intervention can have a lifelong effect — many adults still recall the time a stranger stepped in, which gave them hope.▸ 为何：方案的展开说明（长期影响、带来希望），是第二个支持理由。

问题 + 反应＋
We may hesitate to help children abused by parents in public to avoid possible embarrassment and offense.
▸ 转写：长句缩短（并列句 → 简单句）+ 句式转换（疑问句 → 不定式 to avoid）+ 词性转换（embarrass → embarrassment；offend → offense）
方案 1＋
However, intervention can be helpful and caring.
▸ 转写：信息概括（a world of difference between hurtful criticism and helpful intervention → helpful and caring）+ 连接词 However 表转折
方案 2＋
We are obliged to intervene because children are not strong and old enough for self-protection.
▸ 转写：同义替换（helpless and inexperienced → not strong and old enough）+ 句式转换（have an obligation → are obliged）+ 连接词 because 表原因
方案 3＋
Besides, our intervention may bring hope and have a lifelong impact on their future.
▸ 转写：信息概括（counseling adults recall… → bring hope）+ 同义替换（lifelong effect → lifelong impact）+ 连接词 Besides 表递进

We are obliged to intervene because children are not strong and old enough for self-protection. Besides, our intervention may bring hope and have a lifelong impact on their future.

Why Too Much Running Is Bad for Your Health

研究报告型
原文 · 圈划关键信息（含为何提炼）
① 成果
Marathons can be bad for your health — 80 per cent of competitors suffer kidney injury because of dehydration.▸ 为何：研究成果（结论），研究的核心发现——是概括首句必选。
② 来源
A team led by Professor Chirag Parikh, of Yale University, studied a group in the 2015 Hartford Marathon.▸ 为何：研究来源基本信息（谁 / 哪里），概要须交代研究主体。
③ 过程
They collected blood and urine samples and found that 82% of runners showed Stage 1 Acute Kidney Injury soon after the race.▸ 为何：研究方式与结果（怎么做 + 发现什么）；82% 是数据，但其结论「肾功能受损」须保留。
④ 原因
Potential causes: sustained rise in body temperature, dehydration, or decreased blood flow to the kidneys.▸ 为何：研究结果的潜在原因，解释「为何受伤」，是研究报告型的要点之一。
⑤ 意义
The study raises questions about the effects of repeated activity over time, especially in warm climates.▸ 为何：研究的意义/评论（谨慎态度），作为概要的收尾句。
概括 · 点击揭示（含转写逻辑）
成果 + 来源＋
Researchers of Yale University found marathon might cause kidney injury.
▸ 转写：句子整合（①②合并，来源作主语）+ 同义替换（can be bad for your health → might cause kidney injury）
过程＋
The analysis of blood and urine samples shows most runners' kidneys can't filter waste very well within two days after the race.
▸ 转写：信息概括（blood/urine/markers/82% AKI → most runners' kidneys can't filter waste well）+ 同义替换（soon after → within two days）
原因＋
Rising body temperature, water loss, or slower blood flow may account for the problem.
▸ 转写：同义替换（sustained rise in body temperature → rising body temperature；dehydration → water loss；decreased blood flow → slower blood flow）
意义＋
The finding raises concern about excessive vigorous activities in warm days.
▸ 转写：同义替换（raises questions → raises concern）+ 信息概括（repeated activity over time in warm climates → excessive vigorous activities in warm days）

The analysis of blood and urine samples shows most runners' kidneys can't filter waste very well within two days after the race. Rising body temperature, water loss, or slower blood flow may account for the problem. The finding raises concern about excessive vigorous activities in warm days.

An Unforgettable Experience

It was a hot Sunday morning. John was sitting on a bus. Sitting in front of him was a girl in red. At one stop, an old man got on and walked straight towards them. "I will give my seat to him if he stands beside me," John thought to himself. But he stopped beside the girl in red. She stood up immediately and helped him into her seat. The old man thanked her and she said with a smile, "My pleasure."

The bus went on smoothly. Suddenly, the driver skidded to a stop to avoid a cyclist, and the girl in red fell heavily onto the floor. John dashed over to help her up. But, to his great surprise, he saw a wooden stick where her left leg should have been. A handicapped girl who desperately needed a seat herself gave her seat to the old man!

"Your leg!" murmured John. "Oh, it's okay. It's been a part of me for a long time." She smiled as if nothing had happened. "Please take my seat," John said and reached out to let her sit down.

For the rest of the journey, John could think of nothing but the girl's consideration, confidence, and courage.
