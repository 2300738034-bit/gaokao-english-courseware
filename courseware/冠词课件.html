考点 · 冠词基础

定冠词 · 不定冠词 · 零冠词

a / an / the / Ø
the

定冠词

表“特指”——双方都知道的那个
The plum trees are the first to flower.
梅花是最先开花的。
a / an

不定冠词

表“泛指”——不特定的“一个”
Mount Huangshan is a must to visit!
黄山是一个必游之地！
Ø

零冠词

某些情况下不用冠词
play football（球类不加 the）
by plane（交通方式不加 the）
💡
一句话记忆：the = “那个”（特指），a/an = “一个”（泛指）。翻译 / 语法填空里先判断是特指还是泛指，再考虑固定搭配。
🔑
定冠词 the 的常见“特指”信号：上文提过、谈话双方都知道、独一无二的事物、序数词（the first）、最高级（the best）、乐器（play the piano）。
考点 · 冠词基础

a 还是 an？——看“读音”不看“字母”

a vs an

判断标准是单词开头读音（音素），而不是拼写字母：元音音素开头用 an，辅音音素开头用 a。

用 an（元音音素开头）用 a（辅音音素开头）
an hour 一小时 · an honest boy 诚实的孩子 · an honor 一种荣誉
h 不发音，实际以元音开头		 a useful book · a university · a European · a one-eyed man
元音字母开头，但读音是 /ju:/ 或 /w/（辅音）
⚠️
高考易错点：hour / honest / honor 虽以字母 h 开头，读音却以元音开头 → 用 an；useful / university / united / European / one-eyed 虽以元音字母开头，读音却是辅音 → 用 a。盯读音，别盯字母。
🎯
真题直击（全国卷改错）：Each cafe will have a different theme and an → a unique style.（unique 读 /ju:/，辅音开头，用 a）
考点 1 · 固定短语中的冠词

常考带 the 的短语

Fixed Phrases with “the”
in the middle of在……中间
at the age of在……岁时
at the foot of在……脚下
on the way to在去……的路上
in the habit of有……的习惯
on the right / left在右边 / 左边
at the same time同时
at the top / bottom of在……顶部 / 底部
at the beginning / end of在……开始 / 结束时
in the morning / afternoon / evening在上午 / 下午 / 晚上
by the way顺便问一下
in the form of以……的形式
with the help of在……的帮助下
with the development of随着……的发展
🔑
记忆抓手：这些短语里的 the 是“钉死”的，不能省、不能换。写作翻译里 with the development of…、in the habit of… 是高频套话，写错一处就扣分。
考点 1 · 固定短语中的冠词

常考不带冠词的短语

Fixed Phrases without Article
at college在上大学
at home在家
in advance预先，提前
lose weight减肥
at peace / war处于和平 / 战争状态
in good condition情况良好
on business因公出差
in addition此外，另外
in good order有条不紊
in place of取代
pay attention to注意
🎯
“球类 / 乐器”口诀：球类前不加 the（play football），乐器前要加 the（play the piano）。这是语法填空和改错的高频点。
考点 2 · 有无冠词

有无 the，意思大不同

with / without “the”
无冠词释义加 the释义
in hospital在住院in the hospital在医院
in charge of掌管（主动）in the charge of由……掌管（被动）
in front of在……（外部）前面in the front of在……（内部）前面
in possession of占有in the possession of被……占有
out of question毫无疑问out of the question不可能
⚠️
重点对比：in the front of（在内部前部）vs in front of（在外部前方）——the car 是“坐在车里前排”，the building 是“楼前面”。一个 the 之差，位置完全不同。
考点 3 · 抽象名词具体化

抽象名词前加 a，表示“一种 / 一个”

Concretized Abstract Nouns

某些抽象名词前加 a/an，就“具体化”成可数的人或事物，译为“一个 / 一种 / 一件……”。

例句
He is a success as a teacher but a failure as a father.
他作为教师很成功，作为父亲却是个失败者。（success / failure 具体化为“成功的人 / 失败的人”）
例句
A good knowledge of English is a must in modern society.
良好的英语知识在现代社会是必备条件。（a must = 一件必备的事）
🎯
高频词：a success / a failure / a must / a wonder（奇迹）/ a pleasure / a pity / a shame / a knowledge of…。真题里“Mount Huangshan is a must”正是此考点。
实战 · 上海卷语法填空

真题实战 ①（1–5）

Fill in the blanks
1. It took us about 3 hours to go all ____ way around the Xi'an City Wall.
▸ 答案
the
固定搭配 all the way（一路，全程）。
2. Due to ____ growing popularity of environmentally-related travel, various types of trips are now being classified as ecotourism.
▸ 答案
the
特指“环保旅行日益增长的受欢迎程度”，后接 of 短语限定 → the。
3. While you're in China, Mount Huangshan is ____ must to visit!
▸ 答案
a
抽象名词具体化：a must = 一件必做的事（考点3）。
4. ____ Lincolns enlarged the house to a full two stories in 1856 to meet the needs of their growing family.
▸ 答案
The
the + 姓氏复数 = 一家人（林肯一家）。
5. The plum trees are ____ first to flower even as the snow is melting.
▸ 答案
the
序数词前用 the（the first）。

真题实战 ②（6–10）

Fill in the blanks
6. This style of farming lasted for quite a long time. Then, with ____ rise of science, changes began.
▸ 答案
the
特指“科学的兴起”，of 短语限定 → the。
7. When we got a call saying she was short-listed, we thought it was ____ joke.
▸ 答案
a
泛指“一个玩笑”。
8. Corn production has jumped nearly 125 percent over ____ past 25 years, while rice has increased only 7 percent.
▸ 答案
the
固定表达 the past 25 years（过去 25 年）。
9. Unexpectedly, I'm face-to-face with the gorilla, who begins screaming at ____ top of her lungs.
▸ 答案
the
固定短语 at the top of（在……顶部）。
10. As ____ result, people will eat more food to try to make up for that something missing.
▸ 答案
a
固定短语 as a result（结果，因此）。

真题实战 ⑤（11–20）

Error Correction
11. As is known to all, People's Republic of China is the biggest developing country…
▸ 改正
People's → the People's
专有名词须加 the。
12. …he will also get paid by a week.
▸ 改正
by a week → by the week
“按周计”用 by the week。
13. I have visited many cities since the September 2006.
▸ 改正
the September → September
月份前不加 the。
14. We all know that the man is the most developed animal in the world.
▸ 改正
the man → man
man 泛指“人类”不加冠词。
15. I came by the plane and I had a lovely journey.
▸ 改正
the plane → plane
by + 交通工具，不加 the。
16. What good time we had last night!
▸ 改正
good time → a good time
What a good time！感叹句。
17. We have a music class once week.
▸ 改正
once week → once a week
18. What pity it is that you won't be back before I leave!
▸ 改正
What pity → What a pity
19. It's said that the Americans landed on moon in 1969.
▸ 改正
on moon → on the moon
独一无二天体加 the。
20. …I went to countryside with my father to visit my grandparents.
▸ 改正
countryside → the countryside
实战 · 复盘

冠词高考六大规律

Six Rules
1

a/an 看读音

an hour / a useful book（盯音素，不盯字母）
2

the 表特指

上文提到 / 唯一 / 序数词 / 最高级 / 乐器
3

零冠词

球类、三餐、学科、月份、by + 交通工具
4

the + 姓氏复数

the Lincolns = 林肯一家
5

固定短语

as a result / at the top of / all the way
6

抽象名词具体化

a success / a must / a wonder
🎯
做题顺序：① 先判特指还是泛指（the / a / an）；② 再查读音定 a/an；③ 最后看是否固定搭配或抽象名词具体化。
