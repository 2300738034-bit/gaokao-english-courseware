时态与语态

十六种时态 · 主动语态与被动语态 —— 一对一精讲

板块：语法 · 时态语态
导读

本章要讲什么

Overview

时态语态是高考英语语法的地基：单选、语法填空、翻译、写作，处处都在考。动词的时态由时间（现在 / 过去 / 将来 / 过去将来）与方式（一般 / 进行 / 完成 / 完成进行）交叉，理论上 4×4 = 16 种，但高考真正盯住的只有几类高频时态，外加一个被动语态：

1

一般现在时

习惯 · 真理 · 主将从现
2

一般过去时

过去动作 · 与现在完成区分
3

现在完成时

have/has done · since/for
4

过去完成时

had done · 过去的过去
5

一般将来时

will / be going to / 表将来
6

进行时

现在 / 过去 / 将来进行
7

被动语态

be + 过去分词 · 主动表被动
🧭
两条主线：① 时态 = 时间 × 方式，判断时态先问“什么时候、什么状态”；② 语态 = 主语是动作的执行者（主动）还是承受者（被动）。把这两条线抓住，时态语态就不会乱。
💡
本章最大失分点只有一个：一般过去时 vs 现在完成时。我会专门用一页对比讲透，先把别的基础打牢。
一 · 时态框架

十六种时态，一张表看懂

The 16 Tenses

英语时态 = 时间（横排）× 方式（竖排）。以动词 do 为例，最常用的几个时态已用金底标出：

一般进行完成完成进行
现在I doI am doingI have doneI have been doing
过去I didI was doingI had doneI had been doing
将来I shall/will doI shall be doingI shall have doneI shall have been doing
过去将来I should doI should be doingI should have doneI should have been doing
🎯
高考重点：金底 6 个时态是“必会”，此外现在完成进行时（have been doing）在真题里也出现，表示“从过去一直持续到现在还在做”。其余完成进行、过去将来等，认识即可。
二 · 核心时态

一般现在时

Simple Present
① 经常性 / 习惯性动作
我每天早上 7 点出门上学。
I leave home for school at 7 every morning.
② 客观真理 / 科学事实
地球围绕太阳转。
The earth moves around the sun.
注意：即使主句是过去时，宾语从句里的真理仍用一般现在时。→ Columbus proved that the earth is round.
③ 格言警句
骄者必败。
Pride goes before a fall.
④ 现在的状态 / 能力 / 性格
安英语写得好，但说得不好。
Ann writes good English but does not speak well.
🔑
最常考的其实是“主将从现”：在时间状语从句、条件状语从句里，哪怕说的是将来的事，从句也要用一般现在时。→ When Bill comes, ask him to wait for me.（不是 will come）
二 · 核心时态

一般过去时 vs 现在完成时（重点）

Past vs Perfect

这是本章头号失分点。一句话记住：一般过去时强调“动作本身发生在过去”；现在完成时强调“对现在的影响 / 结果”。

一般过去时现在完成时
强调动作（发生过这件事）结果 / 对现在的影响
时间状语具体：yesterday, last week, in 2013, just now模糊：for, since, so far, ever, never, just, yet
动词延续或短暂动词均可多与延续性动词连用
例子I saw this film yesterday.（昨天看过）I have seen this film.（内容已经知道）
含义对比 · 人还活着吗？
Christine was an invalid all her life.（她已不在人间）
Christine has been an invalid all her life.（她现在还活着）
含义对比 · 还住吗？
Mrs. Smith lived in Kentucky for seven years.（已搬走）
Mrs. Smith has lived in Kentucky for seven years.（仍住着）
⚠️
铁律：句子里一旦出现明确的过去时间状语（yesterday、last night、in 1960…），就绝不能用现在完成时。（✗）Tom has written a letter to his parents last night. →（✓）Tom wrote a letter... last night.
二 · 核心时态

现在完成时

Present Perfect
构成have / has + 过去分词
since 的四种用法
① since + 具体时间点：since 2013
② since + 一段时间 + ago：since five months ago
③ since + 从句：since you left
④ It is + 时间 + since 从句：It is two years since I became a postgraduate.
for vs since
for 说持续多长时间；since 说从什么时候开始。
I have lived here for more than twenty years.
I have lived here since I was born.
注意：并非有 for 就一定用完成时——I worked here for twenty years.（现在不在这工作了）
句型 ① It is/was + the first/second time + that 从句
This is the first time (that) I have heard him sing.
It was the third time that the boy had been late.
句型 ② This is + 最高级 + 名词 + that 从句
This is the best film that I've (ever) seen.
二 · 核心时态

过去完成时

Past Perfect
构成had + 过去分词 → 表示“过去的过去”
① 转述：tell / say / know 等后的宾语从句
She said (that) she had never been to Paris.
② 两个动作，先发生的用过去完成
When the police arrived, the thieves had run away.
③ 意向动词“原本…却未能…”
我们本希望你来的，但你没来。
We had hoped that you would come, but you didn't.
动词：hope, wish, expect, think, intend, mean, suppose
④ 常见时间状语
before / by / until / when / after / once / as soon as
By the time he was twelve, Edison had begun to make a living by himself.
💡
两个动作按顺序接连发生、不强调先后（用 and / then 连接）时，可都用一般过去时：She saw the mouse and screamed. 叙述历史事实也可用一般过去时。
二 · 核心时态

一般将来时（四种表达）

Future
1

will / shall + 动词原形

最通用；shall 用于第一人称
Which paragraph shall I read first?
2

be going to + 不定式

打算 / 计划 / 有迹象
Look at the dark clouds. There is going to be a storm.
3

be + 不定式

按计划 / 正式安排
We are to discuss the report next Monday.
4

be about to + 不定式

马上要做（不与明确将来时间连用）
He is about to leave for Beijing.
🔑
“一般现在时表将来”：① 时刻表已定的安排（The train leaves at six tomorrow morning）；② 时间 / 条件从句中主将从现（I'll write to you as soon as I arrive）。
💡
“现在进行时表将来”：位移动词 come, go, arrive, leave, start, begin, return 用进行时表将来 → I'm leaving tomorrow.
二 · 核心时态

三种进行时

Continuous Tenses
现在进行时 am/is/are + doing
① 正在发生：We are waiting for him.
② 渐变：The leaves are turning yellow.
③ 与 always 连用表感情：You are always changing your mind.（你总改主意）
过去进行时 was/were + doing
① 过去某时正在做（背景动作）：It was raining when they left the station.
② 长动作延续中短动作发生：While Mary was making a dress, she cut her finger.
将来进行时 will + be + doing
将来某时正在做 / 预测：By this time tomorrow, I'll be lying on the beach.
注意：将来进行时不表“意愿”。
🎯
过去进行时是高考的常客：“when + 一般过去时（短动作）” 打断 “过去进行时（长动作/背景）” 这个搭配要一眼认出来。
二 · 核心时态

哪些动词不用于进行时

Non-continuous Verbs

这些动词表示状态、心理、瞬间结果，一般不能用进行时，语法填空里常见：

①

状态动词

have, belong, owe, exist, matter, cost, contain
I have two brothers.（不是 am having）
②

心理动词

know, believe, think, remember, want, need, prefer, love, hate
I know him well.
③

瞬间动词

accept, receive, finish, decide, refuse, complete
I accept your advice.
④

系动词

seem, remain, hear, smell, feel, taste, look, sound
You seem a little tired.
二 · 核心时态

时态的替换（主将从现等）

Tense Shifts
① 一般现在时 → 代替一般将来时
时间 / 条件从句（when, if, unless, as soon as, before…）一律主将从现。
He is going to visit his aunt the day he arrives in Beijing.
② 一般现在时 → 代替一般过去时
“书上说 / 报纸上说”，叙述更生动。
The newspaper says that it's going to be cold tomorrow.
③ 一般现在时 → 代替现在完成时
hear, tell, learn, forget, know 等。
I hear (= have heard) he will go to London.
④ 现在进行时 → 代替一般将来时
位移动词 / 渐变动词 / 瞬间动词 die。
We are leaving for Paris soon. / He is so seriously wounded that he is dying.
主将从现 —— 时间 / 条件从句里，将来也走一般现在时
三 · 被动语态

被动语态的构成

Passive Voice

被动语态 = be + 过去分词，时态全靠 be 的变化来体现，常见 8 种：

时态结构例句
一般现在时am / is / are + doneJapanese is taught in that school.
现在进行时am / is / are + being + doneThe road is being widened.
一般过去时was / were + doneThe house was built last year.
过去进行时was / were + being + doneThe bridge was being repaired.
一般将来时will / shall + be + doneThe report will be discussed.
过去将来时would / should + be + doneHe said the work would be finished.
现在完成时have / has + been + doneThree bridges have been built.
过去完成时had + been + doneThe task had been completed.
情态动词can / may / must / should + be + 过去分词
三 · 被动语态

什么时候用被动语态

When to Use Passive
① 不知道 / 不必指出执行者
Three bridges have been built over the river since 1988.
② 强调承受者（而非执行者）
Japanese is taught in that school.
③ 感官动词：被动时不定式要把 to 还原
We saw him play football. → He was seen to play football.
口诀：主动“see sb. do”，被动“be seen to do”，to 不能丢。
④ 短语动词：被动时别漏掉介词
My younger sister will be taken care of by Grandma.
注意：take care of 里的 of 保留，不能说 be taken care。
📌
“据说 / 据信”固定句型：It is said that… / It is believed that… / It is reported that… / It is well known that…，翻译和写作高频，直接背。
三 · 被动语态

主动表被动 · 哪些动词没有被动

Key Rules

主动形式表被动意义

  • 属性动词：wash, clean, sell, cut, read, write → The book sells well.
  • be to blame / remain / let(出租)：The driver is to blame for the accident.
  • need / want / require / deserve / be worth + doing：The door needs repairing.

没有被动语态的情况

  • 不及物动词：happen, rise, appear, die, take place → The accident happened.
  • 系动词：seem, look, sound, feel → The idea sounds good.
  • 宾语是不定式：She likes to swim.（不说 To swim is liked by her.）
  • 同源宾语：We are living a happy life.
⚠️
易混：rise（不及物，无被动）vs raise（及物，可被动）；happen（无被动）vs 及物动词。→（✓）The price has risen.　（✓）The price has been raised.
四 · 真题自测

真题自测（代表题）

Practice
1 · 现在完成时 + since
The reports went missing in 2012 and nobody ___ them since.
▸ 点击查看答案
C. has seen
考点：since 用现在完成时。
2 · 被动语态（until 从句）
We won't start the work until all the preparations ___.
▸ 点击查看答案
C. have been made
考点：until 从句用现在完成时被动。
3 · 现在完成进行时
— Tony, why are your eyes red? — I ___ up peppers for the last five minutes.
▸ 点击查看答案
D. have been cutting
考点：for the last five minutes 用现在完成进行时。
4 · 主将从现
If we ___ now to protect the environment, we'll live to regret it.
▸ 点击查看答案
C. don't act
考点：if 条件句主将从现。
5 · 过去完成时
I found the lecture hard to follow because it ___ when I arrived.
▸ 点击查看答案
D. had started
考点：过去的过去用过去完成时。
6 · 一般现在时表习惯
On Monday morning it usually ___ me an hour to drive to work.
▸ 点击查看答案
A. takes
考点：usually 用一般现在时。
7 · 现在进行时（打断背景）
Jim ___ a late night film at home when the television went blank.
▸ 点击查看答案
C. was watching
考点：when 打断，用过去进行时。
8 · 被动语态（将来）
We are confident that the environment ___ by our further efforts to reduce pollution.
▸ 点击查看答案
B. will be improved
考点：将来被动。
收尾

本章小结

Summary

时态 · 五句口诀

  • ① 主将从现：时间 / 条件从句里，将来也走一般现在时。
  • ② 一般过去 vs 现在完成：具体时间状语句用过去时；无时间、表影响用完成时。
  • ③ 过去的过去：用过去完成时 had done。
  • ④ since / for：since 看起点，for 看时长。
  • ⑤ 位移动词：进行时表将来。

语态 · 三句口诀

  • ① 被动 = be + done，时态靠 be 变化。
  • ② 主动表被动：need / want / require + doing；书 sells well。
  • ③ 不及物动词、系动词无被动。
⚠️
最易失分的五处：① 有过去时间状语却用了现在完成时；② 时间 / 条件从句误用 will；③ 感官动词被动漏了 to；④ 短语动词被动漏了介词；⑤ rise / happen 等不及物动词硬套被动。
先定时（什么时候）· 再定态（什么状态）· 后定语（主动还是被动）—— 动词三连问，时态语态不丢分
