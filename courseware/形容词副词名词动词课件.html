第 6 节

形容词

The Adjective
形容词主要用来描写、修饰名词或代词，表示人或事物的性质、特征或属性，常用作定语，也可作表语或补语。
1
按结构分类
Simple & Compound Adjectives

形容词分简单形容词（单个词，如 kind 善良的、green 绿色的、interesting 有趣的）与复合形容词（两个以上词，常以连字符连接）两大类。

构成方式例词
数词 + 名词 + 形容词ten-meter-wide 十米宽的 · five-year-old 五岁的
形容词/数词 + 名词 + -edmiddle-aged 中年的 · three-legged 三条腿的 · fifteen-storyed 十五层的
形容词 + V-inggood-looking 好看的 · easy-going 随和的
名词 + V-edman-made 人造的 · snow-covered 白雪覆盖的
副词 + 形容词 + V-edwell-known 著名的 · new-born 新生的
其他sea-sick 晕船的 · home-sick 想家的 · life-long 终身的 · snow-white 雪白的 · ever-green 常青的 · first-rate 一流的
💡
复合形容词主要作定语，其中的名词用单数（a five-year-old girl，不说 five-years-old）。
2
功能及其位置
Functions & Positions
作定语 / 表语
a beautiful girl（定语）
She is beautiful.（表语，位于连系动词后）
作状语（原因 / 伴随）
Curious, the children went into the cave. 出于好奇，孩子们进了山洞。
Most important, all the equipment is homemade. 最重要的是，设备都是国产的。
作补语（主补 / 宾补 / 结果）
The news made her very sad.（宾补）
The farmer sold the oranges green.（宾补）
The father beat the boy black and blue.（结果）
作主语 / 感叹词
Rich and poor were sitting together. 富人和穷人坐在一起。
Old and young joined in the discussion. 老老少少都参加了讨论。
Very good! 很好！ Stupid! 愚蠢！
🔑
形容词作主语往往成对使用，具有名词化特点，指“一类人”（Rich and poor = 富人穷人）。
3
定语形容词的前置与后置
Attributive Position

形容词作定语一般单词前置、短语后置；但修饰复合不定代词（something / anything / nothing / someone / anyone / somewhere / anywhere 等）时必须后置。

I don't find anything interesting in today's newspaper.
今天的报纸没什么有趣的内容。
Someone careful and dutiful is needed to take over the company.
需要一位细心、负责的人来接管公司。
We want to find somewhere quiet and beautiful to live.
想找个安静美丽的地方定居。
4
常作表语的形容词
Predicative-only Adjectives

下列形容词常作表语：afraid, alive, alone, ashamed, asleep, awake, aware, conscious, fond, unable, well。以 a- 开头的形容词如有状语修饰，可作前置定语。

The man was still alive after being trapped for 4 days.
被困四天后那人仍活着。
The half-asleep man will wake at any little sound.
半睡半醒的人会被一点声音吵醒。
She is a very ashamed girl.
她是个很害羞的女孩。
5
形容词的顺序
Word Order

多个不同种类的形容词修饰同一名词时，按下列顺序排列：

限 · 描 · 大 · 形 · 龄 · 颜 · 籍 · 物 · 类 · 名词
类别含义例词
限限定词the, my, three, this
描描绘性brave, beautiful, lovely, nice
大大小高低长短big, large, small, short
形形状round, square
龄年龄 / 新旧 / 年代young, old, new
颜颜色red, blue, green
籍国籍 / 地区 / 出处Chinese, American, Japanese
物物质材料golden, wooden, woolen
类类别 / 用途medical, chemical, writing
a pretty little square old black Japanese wooden writing desk
一张漂亮的日式黑色方形旧木小写字桌
a beautiful short new red Chinese woolen coat
一件漂亮的红色中式短款新羊毛外套
6
主动意义与被动意义的形容词
Active vs Passive
-ing 主动 / -ed 被动
a promising young man 有前途的青年
the reserved tickets 预定的票
后缀提示
主动：-ful / -ive / -ous / -ant
respectful 尊敬的 · pleasant 讨喜的
被动：-ible / -able / -worthy
soluble 可溶的 · trustworthy 可信的
💡
少数形容词主动、被动两可，要看语境：He was sensible of the danger. 他察觉到危险（主动）；Her worry was sensible from her face. 担忧形于色（被动）。
7
最高级的多种表示
Ways to Express Superlative
结构例句
never + such/so + 原级 + 名词I have never seen such a glorious sunrise. 从没见过这么壮观的日出。
as + 原级 + as everHe is as great a philosopher as ever breathed. 有史以来最伟大的哲学家之一。
否定词 + 比较级I have not seen better this year. 今年没见过比这更好的。
比较级 + than any other + 名词He is cleverer than any other student in the class.
否定词 + so/as + 原级 + asNo experience is so valuable as that gained with one's own sweat.
more + adj + than + the + 名词He is more Russian than the Russian. 地地道道的俄罗斯人。
单数名词 + of + 名词复数Confucius is the teacher of teachers. 万师之表。
more than 等强调结构She is more than pretty. 她十分漂亮。 He is all courage itself. 浑身是胆。
8
比较级和最高级的修饰语
Modifiers
as…as 结构前
just, almost / nearly, not half, twice / four times, hundredfold, exactly, not quite, nowhere near, nothing like
— Louise is nearly as tall as her mother.
比较级前
a bit, a head / an inch, somewhat, no / not any, far / much / a lot, hardly any, even / still / rather, any, three times, nothing
— This book is a great deal more interesting.
最高级前后
前：by no means, nothing like, nearly, almost, quite, really, far, much
后：ever, yet（the biggest field yet discovered）
中：second, third, next（the third longest）
⚠️
注意 very 只能修饰原级；修饰比较级用 much / far / even / still / a lot，不能用 very（✗ very more beautiful）。
9
可前置也可后置、含义不同的形容词
Different Meanings
形容词前置（意义）后置（意义）
adopted收养的：an adopted son 养子被采纳的：the proposal adopted
concerned挂念的：a concerned look有关的：all the countries concerned
due应得的：due punishment应付的：the rent due 到期房租
involved复杂的：a fairly involved operation有关的：all the people involved
known有名的：a best-known book已知的：a writer known to us all
present目前的：the present situation在场的：the only person present
responsible有责任心的：a responsible man应负责的：the man responsible 负责人

专项练习

形容词 · 即学即练

单项选择 · 点击展开答案
1. There are ___ students in the small classroom, which makes it very crowded.
A. so manyB. such manyC. so muchD. such a many
▸ 答案
A
“如此多”修饰可数名词复数用 so many；such 后接名词但“many/much/few/little”前用 so。
2. — How about Jim? — He came home ___ and sound.
A. safeB. safelyC. safetyD. safeness
▸ 答案
A
固定搭配 safe and sound（安然无恙），此处 safe 是主语补足语。
3. I couldn't find ___ coat large enough for me in this shop.
A. aB. anC. theD. /
▸ 答案
A
泛指“一件够大的外套”，用 a coat large enough（形容词短语后置）。
4. This broadcast comes to you ___ from the stadium.
A. aliveB. livelyC. liveD. living
▸ 答案
C
live 作副词/形容词表“直播地/现场的”；alive“活着的”、lively“活泼的”、living“活着的”。
5. — Anne is quite unfriendly. — I think she is ___ than unfriendly.
A. shyerB. shierC. more shyD. rather shy
▸ 答案
C
“与其说不友好，不如说害羞”用 more shy than（more A than B = 与其说 B 不如说 A）。
6. ___ more than 200 houses are heated by solar energy in the small town.
A. In the small alone townB. In the small town aloneC. Alone in the small townD. In the alone small town
▸ 答案
B
alone 作“仅仅”解要后置：in the small town alone（仅这个小镇）。
7. Our ___ English short lessons were not difficult at all.
A. first fewB. few firstC. the first fewD. few the first
▸ 答案
A
顺序为 序数词（first）+ 数量词（few）：our first few English short lessons。
8. The house smells as if it hasn't been lived in for years: a ___ house.
A. little white woodenB. little wooden whiteC. white wooden littleD. wooden white little
▸ 答案
A
顺序口诀“描大形龄颜籍物类”：little（大小）+ white（颜色）+ wooden（材料）。
9. He is ___ the cleverest boy in the school.
A. farB. by farC. far theD. by far the
▸ 答案
D
by far 修饰最高级要置于 the 前：by far the cleverest（最最聪明的）。
10. I don't think this film is by far the most boring — I have seen ___ one.
A. a betterB. a worseC. the bestD. the worst
▸ 答案
B
“我看过更无聊的”→ 比较级 a worse one（此处表“一部更差的”）。
11. It is ___ easier to make a plan than to carry it out.
A. muchB. veryC. tooD. quite
▸ 答案
A
修饰比较级用 much / far / even / still，不能用 very。
12. — Is your headache getting ___? — No, it's worse.
A. betterB. badC. lessD. well
▸ 答案
A
“好转”= get better；下句答“更糟了”呼应，正用比较级。
第 7 节

副词

The Adverb
副词以其独特功能丰富语言表达力与灵活性，主要修饰动词、形容词、副词，也可修饰整个句子。
1
副词的功能
Functions
修饰动词 / 形容词 / 副词
He dances happily. 他快乐地跳舞。
an extremely hot day 极其炎热的一天
She runs really quickly. 她跑得真快。
调整语气
Unfortunately, I can't attend the party. 很遗憾，我不能参加。
Certainly, I will help you. 当然，我会帮你。
连接句子 / 表达态度
However, he still kept trying. 然而他仍在坚持。
He elegantly solved the problem. 他优雅地解决了问题（表赞赏）。
位置灵活
Sometimes I go to visit my parents. 有时我去看父母。
I sometimes go to visit my parents. 我有时去看父母。
💡
副词位置不同，语气与强调也不同：句首更强调整体时间/态度，句中语气相对较弱。
2
副词充当的语法成分
Grammatical Roles
作状语
修饰动词、形容词、副词、介词短语、数词、连词乃至全句：The book will come out soon. / Frankly, I don't agree with you.
作表语
位置副词（above, inside, upstairs）或方向副词（up, down, in, out, on, off）：Time is up. 时间到了。/ One of the tyres is down. 一个轮胎没气了。
作定语
多后置：The buildings around are of modern style. / my first day off 休假第一天
也可前置：the up train 北上的列车 / rather a fool
作介词宾语 / 宾补 / 主语
I did not know her until quite recently.（介词宾语）
Please ask her in.（宾补）
Carefully does it. 小心就行。（主语）
专项练习

副词 · 即学即练

单项选择 · 点击展开答案
1. When did you ___ go to the cinema?
A. lastB. lastlyC. at lastD. latest
▸ 答案
A
last 作副词“上一次”；lastly“最后（列举）”；at last“终于”。
2. He is ___ interested in English. He often works ___ into the night.
A. deep; deepB. deeply; deepC. deep; deeplyD. deeply; deeply
▸ 答案
B
抽象意义用 deeply（深深地）；具体意义用 deep（深入地）：work deep into the night 熬夜。
3. Ba Jin is certainly one of the Chinese writers who are ___ read.
A. most widelyB. widely mostC. read most widelyD. most read widely
▸ 答案
A
“被最广泛地阅读”= most widely read（副词修饰过去分词）。
4. He knows little of maths, and ___ of chemistry.
A. still lessB. even moreC. still leastD. no less
▸ 答案
A
“更少”= still less；little 后递进用比较级 less。
5. However, he would ___ have paid ___ for it.
A. gladly; twice as muchB. glad; twice muchC. gladly; as much twiceD. glad; as twice much
▸ 答案
A
“两倍那么多”= twice as much；副词 gladly 修饰动词。
6. Though I tried hard, I couldn't sing ___ he did.
A. half as well asB. as half well asC. half as good asD. as half good as
▸ 答案
A
修饰动词 sing 用副词 well：half as well as（及他一半好）。
7. Could you hold the light ___ to it?
A. a little closerB. a little closeC. closer a littleD. close a little
▸ 答案
A
“再靠近一点”= a little closer（a little 修饰比较级）。
8. He moved away from his parents, and missed them ___ enjoy the exciting life in New York.
A. too much toB. enough toC. very much toD. much so as to
▸ 答案
A
too…to… 结构：太想念父母而无法享受纽约的精彩生活。
9. Those young students were ___ tired, because they had been working for eight hours.
A. not a bitB. hardlyC. not a littleD. nearly
▸ 答案
C
工作八小时“非常累”用 not a little（= very）；not a bit = 一点也不。
10. When hearing the news, the mother was ___ than angry.
A. sorryB. very sorryC. more sorryD. rather sorry
▸ 答案
C
more sorry than angry = 与其说生气，不如说难过。
11. The population of many Alaskan cities has ___ doubled in the past three years.
A. larger thanB. as great asC. as many asD. more than
▸ 答案
D
more than doubled = 不止翻了一番（more than + 动词表程度）。
12. ___ students enough to take this adventure course will learn a lot.
A. Brave enough studentsB. Enough brave studentsC. Students brave enoughD. Students enough brave
▸ 答案
C
enough 修饰形容词须后置：students brave enough（足够勇敢的学生）。
第 8 节

名词

The Noun
名词表示人、事物、地点或抽象概念。分清“可数 / 不可数”与“单数 / 复数”，是名词用法的关键。
1
名词的分类
Classification
专有名词 / 普通名词
专有名词：the Great Wall；姓氏复数表一家：the Greens 格林一家。
普通名词：个体（car, room）· 集体（family, army, government, group）· 物质（fire, steel, air, water, milk）· 抽象（labor, health, life, friendship, patience）。
可数名词复数规则
一般加 s；s/x/sh/ch 加 es；辅音+y → ies（city→cities）；o 结尾有生命加 es（heroes, potatoes, tomatoes）、无生命加 s（zoos, pianos, photos）；f/fe→ves（thief→thieves, wife→wives, life→lives, knife→knives）。
不规则 / 特殊例词
不规则变化tooth→teeth · foot→feet · child→children · mouse→mice · man→men · woman→women
单复数同形Chinese · Japanese · Swiss · works（工厂）· fish · deer · sheep · means
只有复数形式people · cattle · police
以 -s 结尾仍为单数maths · politics · physics · news · the United States · the United Nations
f/fe 例外belief→beliefs · roof→roofs · gulf→gulfs · bamboo→bamboos
2
名词的句法功能
Functions
Recent studies show that we are more productive if we take short breaks.
主语
She is determined to carry on with her education.
宾语
We named the new baby John.
补语
We students need to develop physically and mentally.
同位语
He bought a diamond necklace.
定语
💡
名词作定语一般用单数（paper money, city life, office work）；少数用复数（sports meet, goods train, customs papers）。
3
单位词作修饰语
Partitives
a piece of furniture / chalk / bread一件家具 / 一支粉笔 / 一片面包
a fit of laughter一阵笑声
a gust of wind一阵风
a burst of anger一阵怒火
a round of applause一阵掌声
a bottle of beer / a glass of water一瓶啤酒 / 一杯水
a cake of soap / a bar of chocolate一块肥皂 / 一条巧克力
a crowd of children / a troop of soldiers一群孩子 / 一队士兵
a fleet of ships / a school of fish一支舰队 / 一群鱼
a loaf of bread / a drop of rain一条面包 / 一滴雨水
4
不可数名词的几种用法
Uncountable Nouns

不可数名词一般没有复数、不能用 a/an，需借助量词（✗ informations → information）。但有以下特殊用法：

① 可数化（一份/一杯/一场）
a beer 一瓶啤酒 · a coffee 一杯咖啡 · a rain 一场雨 · a snow 一场雪 · a paper 一张报纸 · a fire 一场大火 · a time 一个时代
② 物质名词复数表种类
foods 各种食物 · wines 各种酒 · waters 水域 · glasses 眼镜 · woods 树林 · sands 沙丘 · fishes 各种鱼
③ 抽象名词可数化
a success 一个成功的人/事 · a surprise 一件意外的事 · a comfort 一个安慰 · an honor 一种荣耀 · a pity 一件遗憾事
Mike is a great honor to my team.
迈克是我们队的骄傲。
A John is waiting for you outside. / We have two Smiths in our class.
专有名词也可数化：一个叫约翰的人 / 两个史密斯。
常以复数出现：trousers, glasses, goods, manners, brains, troops, arms, ashes；动词短语中：take turns, shake hands, make friends, make preparations
复数形式表专门含义
basis→bases · analysis→analyses · crisis→crises · phenomenon→phenomena · medium→media
不规则复数
专项练习

名词 · 即学即练

语法填空 / 改错 · 点击展开答案
1. The rolling sea of clouds will remind you how tiny we ___ (human) are.
▸ 答案
humans
“人类”作泛指用复数 humans。
2. Chinese New Year is a ___ (celebrate) marking the end of winter.
▸ 答案
celebration
a + 名词，celebrate 的名词形式 celebration（庆祝活动）。
3. Filled with ___ (curious), the artist packed his bags and left.
▸ 答案
curiosity
介词 with 后接名词 curiosity（好奇心）。
4. She has turned down several ___ (invitation) to concentrate on her studies.
▸ 答案
invitations
several + 可数名词复数 invitations。
5. Pandas are its top ___ (attract) for tourists like me.
▸ 答案
attraction
top + 名词，attract 的名词形式 attraction（吸引力/景点）。
6. Recent ___ (study) show that exercise improves memory.
▸ 答案
studies
主语为复数概念“研究”，辅音+y 变 studies。
7. He influenced the ___ (develop) of artificial intelligence.
▸ 答案
development
the + 名词，develop 的名词形式 development。
8. This switch has decreased ___ (pollute) in the country's major lakes.
▸ 答案
pollution
不可数名词 pollution（污染），无复数。
9. 改错：Many students say they will talk to their friend or classmates because they're of the same age.
▸ 答案
friend → friends
与 classmates 并列，用复数 friends。
10. 改错：I always assist my parents in doing the dish after meals.
▸ 答案
dish → dishes
“洗碗”= do the dishes（固定用复数）。
11. 改错：Next I broke the eggs into a bowl and beat them quickly with chopstick.
▸ 答案
chopstick → chopsticks
筷子成对，用复数 chopsticks。
12. 改错：Mom taught me some basic step of baking.
▸ 答案
step → steps
some + 可数名词复数 steps（步骤）。
第 9 节

动词

The Verb
根据意义和句法作用，英语动词分为行为动词、连系动词、助动词和情态动词；绝大部分动词有不定式、过去式、过去分词、现在分词四种形式。
1
动词的五种句型结构
Five Sentence Patterns
结构例句
主语 + 动词 (vi)Birds fly. 鸟会飞。 The candle flickered. 烛光摇曳。

动词 + 双宾语
Double Object

接双宾语的动词（间接宾语=人，直接宾语=物）：choose, cook, draw, find, get, order, pick, prepare, save, sing, spare, steal, give, make, show, offer, bring, buy, read, send, take, sell, write, allow, teach, tell, ask, deny。间接宾语可经 to / for 后置。

Mother cooked us a delicious dinner. = Mother cooked a delicious dinner for us.
妈妈为我们做了顿美味的晚餐。
Can you spare me a few minutes?
你能抽出几分钟给我吗？
The choir sang us a beautiful hymn.
合唱团为我们唱了首优美的赞美诗。

常见的动词错误接续
Verb Patterns

掌握动词后接 doing / to do / that 从句 / sb. to do 的正确搭配，是改错与语法填空的高频考点。

正确接续 ✔错误接续 ✘
consider / suggest / advise / finish doingconsider (sb.) to do ✗ · suggest to do ✗ · finish to do ✗
would like / agree / refuse / promise / decide to dowould like doing ✗ · agree doing ✗ · promise doing ✗
allow doing / allow sb. to doallow to do ✗
order / send / tell / ask sb. to doorder / tell to do ✗（缺宾语）
look at / watch / catch sb. doing (do)look at / watch + that 从句 ✗ · catch sb. do ✗
stop / prevent sb. (from) doingstop sb. to do ✗
hope / expect to dohope / expect doing ✗
demand / propose / suggest + that 从句demand / suggest sb. to do ✗
⚠️
特别注意：hope 不能接 sb. to do（✗ hope you to come），只能说 hope that… 或 hope to do；而 wish 可以接 sb. to do。
专项练习

动词 · 即学即练

单项选择 / 填空 · 点击展开答案
1. The Xi'an City Wall was ___ (build) originally to protect the city in the Tang dynasty.
▸ 答案
built
主语 City Wall 与 build 是被动关系，用过去分词 built。
2. After Lincoln was elected, they ___ (sell) most of their furniture.
▸ 答案
sold
与 rented 并列，用一般过去时 sold。
3. It means we have the chance to obtain information about how the moon ___ (construct).
▸ 答案
is constructed
月球“被构建”，一般现在时被动 is constructed。
4. But some students didn't want ___ (wear) the uniform.
▸ 答案
to wear
want to do：want to wear。
5. He decided to give up ___ (smoke).
▸ 答案
smoking
give up doing：放弃吸烟。
6. I found the book ___ (interest).
▸ 答案
interesting
宾语补足语用 -ing 形容词 interesting（书本身“有趣”）。
7. They elected him ___ (chairman) of the department.
▸ 答案
chairman
elect sb. + 名词作宾补，职务名词前不加冠词：elect him chairman。
8. The cake ___ (taste) delicious. I want another piece.
▸ 答案
tastes
taste 作感官系动词，用一般现在时 tastes，无被动。
9. He often ___ (go) fishing on weekends when he was young.
▸ 答案
went
when he was young 表过去习惯，用 went。
10. The teacher asked the students ___ (hand) in their papers.
▸ 答案
to hand
ask sb. to do：让学生交论文。
11. Would you like ___ (have) another cup of coffee?
▸ 答案
to have
would like to do：想再喝一杯。
12. She said that she ___ (leave) for Paris the next week.
▸ 答案
would leave
主句 said 过去时，宾语从句用过去将来时 would leave。
