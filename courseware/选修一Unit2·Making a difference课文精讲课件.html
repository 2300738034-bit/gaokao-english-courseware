课文全文 · 先通读一遍

Island story
绿色高亮 = 好词好句 蓝色高亮 = 语法点 金色下划线 = 固定搭配 · 衔接词

Para 1"When I was a little boy, I loved to go fishing in the sea with my spear," says John Sailike, sadly. "When I leave the island, I'll lose the sea and the happiness of this place. I'll miss the sound of the waves at night... I won't hear it any more."

Para 2John is a fisherman from the Carteret Islands in the South Pacific, a beautiful and peaceful semicircle of white sand, palm trees and crystal clear waters. Each day, he goes out in a small boat to catch fish for his family, while his wife looks after the children and grows vegetables in their garden. The family lives in a wood hut with sand floors and they don't use much electricity, so when the sun goes down, it is usually time for bed. This peaceful, underdeveloped place has a tiny population and only one contact with the outside world — a ship which brings supplies from Bougainville, a large island 86 km away. But all this is about to change, because John, along with 3,000 other people, is leaving his island home. The question is, why?

Para 3Take a closer look at the islands and the reason becomes clear. The Carterets are the victim of global warming. High tides often flood across the villages and wash away people's homes. Salt water from the sea is overflowing into vegetable gardens and destroying fruit trees. It's polluting the fresh water supply too. Islanders are struggling to survive on coconut milk and fish, and children are suffering from malnutrition. The dying trees are home to mosquitoes that spread diseases, like malaria. John and his fellow islanders are facing great challenges, and they know that life on the island is coming to an end.

Para 4Experts predict that the Carterets will be under water in the not-so-distant future. Before then, the islanders will relocate to places like Bougainville and rebuild their lives. This move will provide people with food and shelter, but what will happen to their culture? The islanders have songs for different everyday activities, and traditions and celebrations linked to the sea. They also have their own language, called "Halia". If they coexist with other cultures, this may die out.

Para 5"We're losing our home, our identity, our whole life," says one islander. "We hope the world is listening." It is. In recent years, articles in international newspapers, online videos and an Oscar-nominated documentary film called Sun Come Up have talked about the Carteret Islands. People have supported the islanders and as a result, they have turned their anger and frustration into action. People are also looking for ways to preserve the islanders' way of life and record their language and traditions for future generations.

Para 6Back on the island, John Sailike is getting into his boat for the last time. He's saying goodbye to his home and to his ancestors. He does not know what the future will bring; he does not know if his culture will survive. He is the human face of climate change.

👀
先通读把握主线：卡特里特群岛本是世外桃源，却因全球变暖被海水淹没，岛民被迫迁离家园；更大的痛是文化（语言、传统、身份）的流失。留意三色标注，翻页进入逐段精读。
PART 02 · 逐段精读 ①

Para 1 · 渔夫的不舍与忧伤

A fisherman's sorrow
"When I was a little boy, I loved to go fishing① in the sea with my spear②," says John Sailike, sadly③. "When I leave the island④, I'll lose the sea and the happiness of this place. I'll miss the sound of the waves at night... I won't hear it any more."
①go fishing固定搭配
点此查看 讲解 · 例句 · 拓展＋
讲解go fishing 去钓鱼；go + doing 表示从事某项活动（尤其户外/娱乐）。
例句They often go fishing at weekends.
他们周末常去钓鱼。
拓展go swimming / go shopping / go hiking / go camping 等。
高考考点go doing 结构（语法填空、翻译高频）。
②spear好词好句
点此查看 讲解 · 例句 · 拓展＋
讲解spear n. 鱼叉、矛；v. 用矛/鱼叉刺。
例句He caught the fish with a spear.
他用鱼叉叉住了鱼。
拓展go fishing with my spear 带着鱼叉去捕鱼（传统渔猎方式）。
高考考点spear 为课标词，认识即可（阅读理解推测词义）。
③sadly / miss / the sound of the waves好词好句
点此查看 讲解 · 例句 · 拓展＋
讲解sadly 难过地（副词修饰 says）；miss 想念、怀念；the sound of the waves 海浪声。
例句She misses the sound of the waves at night.
她怀念夜晚的海浪声。
拓展miss 一词多义：想念 / 错过（miss the bus）/ 漏掉。
高考考点miss 一词多义（阅读、完形高频）；sadly 副词用法。
④When I leave the island, I'll lose …语法点
点此查看 讲解 · 例句 · 拓展＋
讲解when 引导时间状语从句，主句用一般将来时（I'll lose），从句用一般现在时表将来（I leave）。
例句When he arrives, I'll tell him the news.
他到了我就告诉他这个消息。
拓展时间/条件状语从句中「主将从现」：if / when / as soon as 等。
高考考点「主将从现」——语法填空、翻译必考。
PART 02 · 逐段精读 ②

Para 2 · 海岛上的平静生活

A peaceful, underdeveloped place
John is a fisherman from the Carteret Islands in the South Pacific, a beautiful and peaceful semicircle of white sand, palm trees and crystal clear waters①. Each day, he goes out in a small boat to catch fish for his family, while his wife looks after the children② and grows vegetables in their garden. The family lives in a wood hut with sand floors③ and they don't use much electricity, so when the sun goes down, it is usually time for bed. This peaceful, underdeveloped④ place has a tiny population④ and only one contact with the outside world — a ship which brings supplies⑤ from Bougainville, a large island 86 km away. But all this is about to change, because John, along with⑥ 3,000 other people, is leaving⑦ his island home. The question is, why?
①a semicircle of white sand, palm trees and crystal clear waters好词好句
点此查看 讲解 · 例句 · 拓展＋
讲解semicircle 半圆形；crystal clear 清澈透明的。整句用三个并列名词短语描绘海岛，画面感极强。
例句The island is a semicircle of white sand, palm trees and crystal clear waters.
这座岛是白色沙滩、棕榈树和清澈海水的半圆形。
拓展描写景色的排比结构；crystal clear 常形容水、天空。
高考考点景物描写句型（读后续写 / 写作可直接模仿）。
②while his wife looks after the children语法点
点此查看 讲解 · 例句 · 拓展＋
讲解while 引导时间状语从句，表「与此同时」；前后两个动作同时进行（他出海，妻子照顾孩子）。
例句He cooked dinner while his wife cleaned the house.
他做晚饭，而妻子打扫房子。
拓展while 也可表「尽管/对比」（= although / whereas）。
高考考点while 的多义（当…时 / 然而 / 尽管）——阅读、单选高频。
③wood hut with sand floors / look after好词好句
点此查看 讲解 · 例句 · 拓展＋
讲解wood hut with sand floors 沙子地面的木屋；look after 照顾、照料（= take care of）。
例句She looks after her younger brother every day.
她每天照顾弟弟。
拓展look after = care for = take care of；「with + 名词」表伴随特征。
高考考点look 相关短语（look after / for / up / forward to）——完形、单选高频。
④underdeveloped / a tiny population好词好句
点此查看 讲解 · 例句 · 拓展＋
讲解underdeveloped 欠发达的、不发达的（under- 不足 + developed 发达的）；a tiny population 人口稀少。
例句It is an underdeveloped region with a tiny population.
这是个欠发达、人口稀少的地区。
拓展under- 前缀：underdeveloped / underground / underestimate。
高考考点under- 前缀构词法（阅读推测词义常考）。
⑤a ship which brings supplies语法点
点此查看 讲解 · 例句 · 拓展＋
讲解which 引导定语从句，修饰 ship；supplies（复数）供应品、补给。
例句The ship which brings supplies arrives once a month.
运送补给的船每月来一次。
拓展supply v./n. 供应；supply sb. with sth. 为某人提供某物。
高考考点which 引导定语从句（语法填空、翻译高频）；supply 一词多义。
⑥along with固定搭配
点此查看 讲解 · 例句 · 拓展＋
讲解along with 连同、和……一起（= together with）。注意：along with 后接的成分不影响谓语单复数。
例句John, along with 3,000 other people, is leaving the island.
约翰连同 3000 名其他人一起离开海岛。
拓展类似结构：together with / as well as / with（就远原则）。
高考考点就远原则——主谓一致（语法填空、改错高频）。
⑦is leaving语法点
点此查看 讲解 · 例句 · 拓展＋
讲解现在进行时表将来：leave / come / go / arrive 等位移动词，用 be + doing 表示「即将发生」的计划、安排。
例句He is leaving for Beijing tomorrow.
他明天动身去北京。
拓展be about to do 即将（后文 "is about to change"）。
高考考点现在进行时表将来（单选、翻译高频）。
PART 02 · 逐段精读 ③

Para 3 · 全球变暖的灾难

The victim of global warming
Take a closer look at① the islands and the reason becomes clear. The Carterets are the victim② of global warming②. High tides often flood③ across the villages and wash away③ people's homes. Salt water from the sea is overflowing④ into vegetable gardens and destroying④ fruit trees. It's polluting④ the fresh water supply⑤ too. Islanders are struggling to survive⑥ on coconut milk and fish, and children are suffering from⑦ malnutrition⑦. The dying trees are home to⑧ mosquitoes that spread⑧ diseases, like malaria⑧. John and his fellow islanders are facing great challenges⑨, and they know that life on the island is coming to an end⑨.
①take a closer look at固定搭配
点此查看 讲解 · 例句 · 拓展＋
讲解take a closer look at 更仔细地看一看（比较级 closer 修饰）。
例句Take a closer look at the photo and you'll see it.
仔细看看照片，你就会发现。
拓展have a look at 看一看；look closely at 仔细看。
高考考点take a look at / have a look at（翻译、写作高频）。
②the victim of global warming好词好句
点此查看 讲解 · 例句 · 拓展＋
讲解victim 受害者、牺牲品；global warming 全球变暖。
例句These islands are the victims of global warming.
这些岛屿是全球变暖的受害者。
拓展a victim of … ……的受害者；fall victim to 沦为……的牺牲品。
高考考点环境话题核心词（阅读、写作高频）。
③flood across … / wash away好词好句
点此查看 讲解 · 例句 · 拓展＋
讲解flood v. 淹没（n. 洪水）；wash away 冲走。
例句High tides flooded the village and washed away people's homes.
涨潮淹没了村庄，冲走了人们的家园。
拓展high tide 涨潮 / low tide 退潮；flood n.&v.
高考考点flood 词性活用（名词动词兼用）——语法填空常考。
④overflow / destroy / pollute好词好句
点此查看 讲解 · 例句 · 拓展＋
讲解overflow 溢出；destroy 毁坏；pollute 污染。三个动词并列，层层递进写海水之害。
例句Salt water is overflowing into the gardens, destroying the trees and polluting the water.
咸水涌入菜园，毁坏树木，污染水源。
拓展pollution n. 污染；destroy = ruin；overflow = flow over。
高考考点pollute → pollution 词形转换（语法填空常考）。
⑤the fresh water supply好词好句
点此查看 讲解 · 例句 · 拓展＋
讲解fresh water supply 淡水供应；supply n. 供应（量）。
例句The fresh water supply has been polluted.
淡水供应已被污染。
拓展fresh water 淡水 / salt water 咸水；supply v. 提供。
高考考点supply 名词/动词用法（概要填空、翻译高频）。
⑥struggle to survive固定搭配
点此查看 讲解 · 例句 · 拓展＋
讲解struggle to do 艰难地做、挣扎着做；survive 生存、幸存。
例句They are struggling to survive on limited food.
他们靠着有限的食物艰难求生。
拓展struggle for 为……而斗争；survivor n. 幸存者。
高考考点struggle to do（翻译、写作高频）；survive 一词多义。
⑦suffer from malnutrition好词好句
点此查看 讲解 · 例句 · 拓展＋
讲解suffer from 遭受、患（病）；malnutrition 营养不良（mal- 坏 + nutrition 营养）。
例句Many children are suffering from malnutrition.
许多孩子正遭受营养不良之苦。
拓展mal- 前缀（坏）：malnutrition / malfunction；nutrition 营养。
高考考点suffer / suffer from 辨析；mal- 前缀构词。
⑧be home to / spread diseases / malaria固定搭配
点此查看 讲解 · 例句 · 拓展＋
讲解be home to 是……的家园/栖息地；spread diseases 传播疾病；malaria 疟疾。
例句The dying trees are home to mosquitoes that spread malaria.
枯死的树木成了传播疟疾的蚊子的栖息地。
拓展be home to（= be the home of）；spread — spread — spread 不规则动词。
高考考点spread 不规则过去式/分词（语法填空常考）。
⑨challenges / come to an end好词好句
点此查看 讲解 · 例句 · 拓展＋
讲解challenge 挑战；face great challenges 面临巨大挑战；come to an end 结束、走到尽头。
例句They are facing great challenges, and life on the island is coming to an end.
他们面临巨大挑战，岛上的生活即将走到尽头。
拓展challenging adj. 有挑战性的；face / meet a challenge。
高考考点challenge 词形转换（challenging）——语法填空高频。
PART 02 · 逐段精读 ④

Para 4 · 迁离与文化的困境

Relocation & culture
Experts predict① that the Carterets will be under water in the not-so-distant future②. Before then, the islanders will relocate③ to places like Bougainville and rebuild③ their lives. This move will provide people with④ food and shelter, but what will happen to their culture⑤? The islanders have songs for different everyday activities, and traditions and celebrations linked to⑥ the sea. They also have their own language, called "Halia"⑦. If they coexist with other cultures⑧, this may die out⑨.
①predict / relocate / rebuild好词好句
点此查看 讲解 · 例句 · 拓展＋
讲解predict 预测；relocate 迁移、搬迁；rebuild 重建（re- 再 + build）。
例句Experts predict that the islanders will relocate and rebuild their lives.
专家预测岛民将搬迁并重建生活。
拓展prediction n. 预测；re- 前缀：rebuild / return / recall。
高考考点re- 前缀构词（rebuild / relocate）——阅读推断词义。
②in the not-so-distant future固定搭配
点此查看 讲解 · 例句 · 拓展＋
讲解in the not-so-distant future 在不久的将来（= in the near future）。
例句The islands will be under water in the not-so-distant future.
这些岛屿在不久的将来就会被水淹没。
拓展in the near future / in the distant future 在遥远的将来。
高考考点「在不久的将来」的多样表达（概要填空、写作高频）。
③provide people with food and shelter固定搭配
点此查看 讲解 · 例句 · 拓展＋
讲解provide sb. with sth. 为某人提供某物（= provide sth. for sb.）；shelter 遮蔽、住所。
例句The move will provide the islanders with food and shelter.
这次搬迁将为岛民提供食物和住所。
拓展provide sb. with sth. / provide sth. for sb. / offer sb. sth.
高考考点provide 的双宾语结构（翻译、语法填空高频）。
④culture / coexist好词好句
点此查看 讲解 · 例句 · 拓展＋
讲解culture 文化；coexist 共存（co- 共同 + exist 存在）。
例句Different cultures can coexist peacefully.
不同文化可以和平共存。
拓展co- 前缀：coexist / cooperate / co-worker。
高考考点culture / cultural 词形转换；co- 前缀构词。
⑤traditions and celebrations linked to the sea语法点
点此查看 讲解 · 例句 · 拓展＋
讲解linked to the sea 过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰 traditions and celebrations（= which are linked to the sea）。
例句They have traditions and celebrations linked to the sea.
他们有与大海相关的传统和庆典。
拓展过去分词作定语（表被动/完成）：books written in English。
高考考点过去分词作后置定语（语法填空、翻译高频）。
⑥their own language, called "Halia"语法点
点此查看 讲解 · 例句 · 拓展＋
讲解called "Halia" 过去分词短语作后置定语（= which is called "Halia"）。
例句They speak their own language, called Halia.
他们讲自己的语言，叫哈里亚语。
拓展同结构：a film called Sun Come Up（见 Para 5）。
高考考点过去分词作定语——本课高频语法点。
⑦If they coexist with other cultures语法点
点此查看 讲解 · 例句 · 拓展＋
讲解if 引导条件状语从句（一般现在时表将来），主句用情态动词 may 表推测。
例句If they coexist with other cultures, their language may die out.
如果他们与其他文化共存，他们的语言可能会消亡。
拓展if 从句「主将从现」/「主情从现」。
高考考点if 条件句（主将从现）——语法填空、单选高频。
⑧die out固定搭配
点此查看 讲解 · 例句 · 拓展＋
讲解die out 灭绝、消失（指物种、语言、文化等逐渐消亡）。
例句Many traditional customs are dying out.
许多传统习俗正在消失。
拓展die 相关短语：die out / die off / die down（逐渐平息）。
高考考点die 相关短语辨析（完形、单选高频）。
PART 02 · 逐段精读 ⑤

Para 5 · 世界的回应

The world is listening
"We're losing our home, our identity①, our whole life," says one islander. "We hope the world is listening." It is. In recent years, articles in international newspapers, online videos and an Oscar-nominated② documentary film called Sun Come Up② have talked about the Carteret Islands. People have supported the islanders and as a result③, they have turned their anger and frustration into④ action. People are also looking for ways to preserve⑤ the islanders' way of life and record⑥ their language and traditions for future generations⑥.
①identity好词好句
点此查看 讲解 · 例句 · 拓展＋
讲解identity 身份、认同感；lose our identity 失去我们的身份/认同。
例句They fear losing their identity and culture.
他们害怕失去自己的身份和文化。
拓展identify v. 识别、认同；cultural identity 文化认同。
高考考点identity / identify 词形转换（语法填空高频）。
②Oscar-nominated … called Sun Come Up好词好句
点此查看 讲解 · 例句 · 拓展＋
讲解Oscar-nominated 获奥斯卡提名的；called Sun Come Up 过去分词作定语（名叫《太阳升起》）。
例句An Oscar-nominated film called Sun Come Up told their story.
一部名为《太阳升起》的奥斯卡提名影片讲述了他们的故事。
拓展nominate v. 提名；documentary film 纪录片。
高考考点过去分词作定语（called）——本课高频；-nominated 复合词。
③as a result衔接词
点此查看 讲解 · 例句 · 拓展＋
讲解as a result 结果、因此（衔接词，表结果）。
例句People supported them, and as a result, they turned anger into action.
人们支持他们，结果他们把愤怒化为了行动。
拓展as a result of 由于；therefore / thus 因此。
高考考点as a result / as a result of 辨析（单选、写作高频）。
④turn their anger and frustration into action固定搭配
点此查看 讲解 · 例句 · 拓展＋

Grammar Review
1现在进行时表将来

2when 引导时间状语从句

3while 引导时间状语从句

He doesn't know what will come.

PART 05 · Comprehension ①

阅读理解 · 6 道问答讲评

Comprehension Q&A
1Why is John Sailike sad?
点此查看答案 · 解析＋
Because he has to leave the island where he has lived since childhood. He will lose the sea and the happiness of the place, and he will miss the sound of the waves at night.
【定位】第一段 John 的自白：离开小岛＝失去大海、快乐与夜里的海浪声。
2What is life like on the Carteret Islands?
点此查看答案 · 解析＋
Simple, peaceful and underdeveloped: John goes fishing in a small boat, his wife looks after the children and grows vegetables, and the family lives in a wood hut with sand floors, using little electricity.
【归纳】第二段：捕鱼、种菜、住沙地木屋、几乎不用电，日出而作日落而息。
3What first impression of the islands does the writer give?
点此查看答案 · 解析＋
A beautiful, peaceful and unspoiled place — a semicircle of white sand, palm trees and crystal clear waters, with a tiny population and little contact with the outside world.
【归纳】第二段前半先写「世外桃源」般的美，后文才揭示危机（欲扬先抑的写法）。
4What might happen when the islanders relocate?
点此查看答案 · 解析＋
They will get food and shelter, but their culture — songs, traditions, celebrations and their Halia language — may die out if they coexist with other cultures.
【定位】第四段 "what will happen to their culture? … this may die out."
5How have people helped the islanders?
点此查看答案 · 解析＋
Through international newspaper articles, online videos and the Oscar-nominated documentary Sun Come Up, people have raised awareness, supported the islanders, turned their anger and frustration into action, and looked for ways to preserve their way of life and record their language and traditions.
【定位】第五段：媒体传播 → 公众支持 → 付诸行动 → 保护文化。
6How would you respond to John Sailike's worries in the last paragraph?
点此查看参考思路＋
开放性题，无标准答案。示例：I would tell him that the world is listening, and that his culture will be recorded and remembered for future generations; though he is leaving home, he is the human face of climate change and his story inspires people to act.
【答题技巧】先回应情绪（理解/安慰），再结合课文主旨（行动带来改变 Making a difference）给理由，用 I would tell him / I'd reassure him 组织。
🎯
答问技巧：先定位再答题；归纳题抓段落主旨（第 2、3 题），推断题看上下文（第 6 题），开放题要有观点 + 理由。
